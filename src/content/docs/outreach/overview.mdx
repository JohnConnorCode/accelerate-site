Outreach is campaigns, email templates, and reactivation of past demand. The screens are Campaigns, Email Studio, Delivery Runs, and Revenue Recovery.

- [Campaigns](/docs/outreach/campaigns). Approve a version once. Automation then runs inside sender, audience, cadence, limit, and stop rules.
- [Email Studio](/docs/outreach/email-studio). Edit live copy, inspect what was sent, compose a direct follow-up.
- [Revenue Recovery](/docs/outreach/recovery). Reviewed past relationships become governed opportunities.

## Campaigns

A campaign is versioned copy plus a membership list plus a send policy. Activation is an approved action. Sequences stop when someone replies. Contacts who unsubscribed or asked to be left alone are refused before a send is attempted. Each send has an idempotency key and a receipt.

## Email Studio

Templates for transactional and marketing mail live here. Drafts are protected. Publishing a revision is explicit. Preview and test sends use the same rendering path as production.

## Recovery

Stale opportunities, no-shows, and past quotes stay in a working set. Recovery playbooks propose work into the same [approval](/docs/command-center/approvals) queue as everything else.
