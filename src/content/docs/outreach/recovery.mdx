A quote that went quiet eight months ago is not necessarily a dead lead, it might just be a business that got busy, and Revenue Recovery is where you find that opportunity again instead of leaving it buried. Open `/admin/recovery` to review past opportunities, no-shows, and quotes that may genuinely warrant another conversation.

## Review the proposed audience

1. Preview the working set before staging work.
2. Inspect the people included and why they qualify. Check their current opportunity and conversation before approving outreach.
3. Review exclusions. Suppressed contacts, people already in an active opportunity and existing recovery memberships should not be added again.
4. Confirm the proposed message and the exact playbook version you intend to approve.

An empty preview may mean every candidate was excluded. Inspect the eligibility reasons before widening the audience.

## Approve and inspect the result

Approved memberships use the campaign workflow and its send limits and stop rules. Use [Campaigns](/docs/outreach/campaigns) to understand how those rules affect execution. Assistant-proposed changes are reviewed through [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals).

After approval, inspect the resulting membership and execution status. Approval does not establish that a message was sent. Check the [action result](/docs/start/receipts) before retrying failed or uncertain work.

## Change a playbook

Review the new version before using it. Check that every template variable can be filled from the candidate records. Missing information needs correction or exclusion; it is not permission to invent a personalized detail.
