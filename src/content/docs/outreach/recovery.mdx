Open `/admin/recovery`. The working set is stale opportunities, no-shows, and past quotes. Preview always precedes staging. Mail does not leave until you approve an exact playbook version.

## Eligibility

Recovery excludes suppressed contacts, people already in an active opportunity, and people already in a recovery membership. Templates may only use variables the campaign executor can resolve. A preview that looks empty is often an eligibility filter, not a missing list.

## From preview to send

Approve the playbook version. Memberships are created as campaign members under the same stop rules as any other [campaign](/docs/outreach/campaigns). Assistant-proposed recovery still lands in [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals).
