An inquiry is useful only when someone understands the request and takes responsibility for the next step. Sources keeps website submissions, chat handoffs and other intake records available for review, so your team can check the person’s identity before continuing the relationship.

## Leads and chat

`/admin/leads` holds submissions from your forms; `/admin/chat-leads` holds handoffs from the public assistant. Qualifying the inquiry happens right here, and resolving its identity into an actual [Contact](/docs/contacts) is a deliberate review step afterward, not something that happens automatically the moment a form is submitted.

## Subscribers, partners, and grades

Newsletter and resource subscribers, referral partner applications, and website grader intake each keep their own list, sharing the same tenant boundary as everything else in your workspace. A new row here waits for a person to confirm a merge before it becomes part of your real customer record.


## Turn an AI readiness assessment into a follow-up

A visitor can complete the public [AI readiness assessment](/ai-readiness), review the preview, and save a report with a practical action plan. Once saved, the same report link and PDF remain available on retry, so a connection interruption does not replace the visitor's result.

Open Resources to review assessment activity and the saved contact details, then follow the linked customer work through the normal workspace. Scores summarize the visitor's answers; they are starting points for a conversation about one useful pilot. An optional website check inspects the public homepage and reports when a destination cannot be reached safely.

The requested report uses the workspace's recorded email delivery service. If delivery needs attention, the saved web report remains available and Activity holds the delivery evidence. Optional marketing permission is saved separately; saving a report does not enroll the visitor in a follow-up sequence.
