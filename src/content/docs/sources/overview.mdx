Sources are the intake ledgers that are not yet the canonical contact: direct leads, chat inquiries, subscribers, partner applications, and website grades.

How form and chat intake works is in [Leads and chat](/docs/sources/leads).

## Leads and chat

`/admin/leads` and `/admin/chat-leads` hold submissions from forms and the public assistant. Qualification happens here. Identity resolution into [Contacts](/docs/contacts) is a review step.

## Subscribers, partners, grades

Newsletter and resource subscribers, referral partner applications, and website grader intake each have their own list. They share the same tenant boundary. A new row waits for someone to confirm a merge.
