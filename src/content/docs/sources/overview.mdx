A form submission, a chat transcript, and a partner application all start life as the same kind of thing: information about someone who is not yet a canonical contact, and might not even be a real match for anyone in your system. Sources are the intake ledgers that hold that in-between state, direct leads, chat inquiries, subscribers, partner applications, and website grades, each waiting for someone to actually confirm who it belongs to.

## Leads and chat

`/admin/leads` holds submissions from your forms; `/admin/chat-leads` holds handoffs from the public assistant. Qualifying the inquiry happens right here, and resolving its identity into an actual [Contact](/docs/contacts) is a deliberate review step afterward, not something that happens automatically the moment a form is submitted.

## Subscribers, partners, and grades

Newsletter and resource subscribers, referral partner applications, and website grader intake each keep their own list, sharing the same tenant boundary as everything else in your workspace. A new row here waits for a person to actually confirm a merge before it becomes part of your real customer record.
