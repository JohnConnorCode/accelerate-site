# Manage your subscription

Your workspace account keeps your plan, renewal date, invoices, and billing actions together. Start from the workspace’s **Plans** page, then use your customer account whenever you need to review or change billing.

## Subscribe

1. Open the workspace’s Plans page.
2. Choose a plan and continue to secure Stripe Checkout.
3. Sign in or create an account if asked.
4. Complete payment in Stripe. You return to your account when checkout finishes.

Your plan renews automatically at the interval shown on the plan. The account page is the source for the current subscription status and next renewal date.

## Manage payment details

Open **Manage payment method** in your account. Stripe’s secure billing portal handles card and payment details, so the workspace never asks you to paste them into its own form. The portal also provides Stripe’s available invoice and payment actions for your account.

## Change or cancel

Choose **Schedule change** to move to another active plan at the next renewal. Plan changes keep the current currency and do not change the current billing period.

Choose **Cancel at renewal** to stop the next renewal. You keep access through the current period. If you change your mind, choose **Keep subscription** before the renewal date.

The account shows the pending plan or cancellation so you can see what will happen next.

## If something looks wrong

- **Checkout did not finish:** return to Plans and try again. An incomplete checkout does not count as an active subscription.
- **Your subscription is missing:** refresh the account page. If it remains missing after a completed payment, contact the workspace owner with the approximate checkout time.
- **A payment is past due:** update your payment method in the Stripe portal and check the latest invoice status.
- **An invoice is unavailable:** the subscription can remain visible while invoice history is being refreshed. Try again later or contact the workspace owner.

For account access problems, use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. For a billing question that is not answered here, contact the workspace owner or the support address shown on your account page.
