Workspace is tenancy, setup, settings, integrations, and the Feature Board.

- [Integrations](/docs/workspace/integrations). Providers, module switches, and the MCP key.
- [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup). Connect, verify, and operate. Status reflects this deployment.
- [Settings](/docs/workspace/settings). Notification preferences and operating configuration.

## Tenants and setup

`/admin/tenants` provisions workspaces and memberships. `/admin/setup` is the readiness list: schema, providers, mail, calendar, and AI. A check that is not ready is shown as not ready. Setup does not expose secrets.

## Integrations and modules

`/admin/integrations` is the connector catalog and the module switches. Provider facts are evidence. A planned connector does not appear installed. See [Modules](/docs/start/modules).

## Settings and the Feature Board

Operating preferences live in Settings. The Feature Board is the delivery backlog the public [roadmap](/roadmap) renders from the same manifest. This page does not restate that board.
