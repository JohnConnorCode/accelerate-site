Before any of the daily workflow works, something has to provision your business, connect your tools, and confirm those connections actually function, and that groundwork is what Workspace covers: tenancy, setup, settings, integrations, and the Feature Board that tracks what's still being built.

[Integrations](/docs/workspace/integrations) is where providers, module switches, and your MCP key live. [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) is where you connect, verify, and operate, with status that reflects this specific deployment rather than a generic checklist. [Settings](/docs/workspace/settings) holds notification preferences and the rest of your operating configuration.

## Tenants and setup

`/admin/tenants` provisions workspaces and memberships; `/admin/setup` is the readiness list covering schema, providers, mail, calendar, and AI. A check that genuinely is not ready gets shown as not ready, plainly, and Setup never exposes a secret to tell you that.

## Integrations and modules

`/admin/integrations` is both the connector catalog and the module switches in one place. Provider facts shown there are evidence of an actual connection, not a promise: a planned connector never shows up looking installed when it isn't. See [Modules](/docs/start/modules) for how enabling or disabling one actually behaves.

## Settings and the Feature Board

Operating preferences live in Settings. The Feature Board is the delivery backlog the public [roadmap](/roadmap) renders directly from, so this page points at it rather than duplicating a second, drifting copy of the same list.
