Open `/admin/proposals`. Create a draft, attach it to the client and opportunity, and keep totals inside the published catalog. A total that does not add up is rejected before send.

## Send

Sending issues an unguessable public token. The recipient views, accepts, or declines at that URL. Each of those events writes an audit row. The proposal body is not stored in the audit payload.

## After send

Viewed and pending proposals appear on [Today](/docs/command-center/today) and in [Inbox](/docs/command-center/inbox). Accepting does not invent a won stage by itself. Move the [pipeline](/docs/pipeline/board) card when the evidence is there, or approve the assistant's stage proposal.
