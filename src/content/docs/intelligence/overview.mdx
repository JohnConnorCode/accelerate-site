Intelligence is the assistant, its traces, knowledge retrieval, and source-to-revenue analytics.

## AI Workspace

`/admin/ai` is where you ask, inspect runs, and review capabilities. How to use that screen is in [AI Workspace](/docs/intelligence/workspace). Context is bounded and sourced. Invented citations degrade to a partial answer. Writes and outbound actions enter the [approval](/docs/command-center/approvals) queue and use the same services as the UI.

The [AI tool reference](/docs/intelligence/tools) lists every registered tool from the registry. That list is not copied into prose.

## Analytics

`/admin/analytics` is the funnel: source, owner, campaign, stage, communication, forecast, and data-quality signals. Counts come from the same records the rest of the workspace uses.
