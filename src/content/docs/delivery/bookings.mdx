---

Open `/admin/bookings`. Rows are qualification, scheduled calls, attendance, and attributed revenue from those meetings.

Calendar sync is on only when the Google or Calendly connection is ready in [Setup](/docs/workspace/setup). Calendly stays optional and off until a workspace turns it on. A booking without an attributed contact is intake work for [Contacts](/docs/contacts), not a finished meeting record.
