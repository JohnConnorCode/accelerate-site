Delivery is what happens after a deal is won: client records, bookings, content operations, and the resource library.

- [Clients](/docs/delivery/clients). Active accounts, retainer scope, and delivery status.
- [Bookings](/docs/delivery/bookings). Qualification, scheduled calls, attendance, and attributed revenue.
- [Content](/docs/delivery/content). Editorial briefs and publishing status on a shared calendar.
- [Resources](/docs/delivery/resources). Gated downloads that attach to contacts.

## Clients

Active accounts, retainer scope, and delivery status live at `/admin/clients`. That screen is the delivery view of people and companies you already have.

## Bookings

Meeting and booking records, including calendar sync when that integration is on. Calendly stays optional and off until a workspace turns it on.

## Content and resources

Content operations holds briefs and publishing status. Resources holds gated downloads. Both attach to contacts and campaigns when those records exist, so a download creates intake.
