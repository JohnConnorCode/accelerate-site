A brief that moves to "Published" in your tracker means nothing if the actual page never went live, which is exactly the gap this screen exists to close. Open `/admin/content` to manage briefs and editorial work; the Content Calendar offers board and list views, with columns that represent your workspace's real publishing stages.

## Create and track an item

1. Select **New Content** and enter the details available in the form. Give the item a title that identifies the piece, such as “September customer onboarding email”.
2. Save it and confirm it appears in the expected column.
3. Open the item to update its details. Move it when the work has reached the next stage.
4. Switch to list view if that makes the queue easier to review. Reload after a change to confirm it persisted.

A status change records editorial progress. It does not, by itself, publish a page or send a campaign. Check the destination separately before marking a piece published.

## Review a generated draft

Check names, dates, prices, statistics and source links against the material used for the brief. Replace unsupported claims before publication. An AI draft is working copy until someone has reviewed it.

## When an update does not stick

Reload and inspect the saved item before repeating the change. Record the item title, previous state and intended state for the technical owner. Avoid creating a second item to work around a failed edit.
