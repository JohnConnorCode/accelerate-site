Open `/admin/clients`. This is the delivery view of companies you already have: active flag, retainer scope, and status. People still live under [Contacts](/docs/contacts).

Won pipeline deals should have a client row when delivery work starts. Open a client for the people, opportunities, and conversations attached to that account.
