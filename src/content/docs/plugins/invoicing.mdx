Invoices live in this workspace's database with their own numbers and a branded
customer page. Stripe only moves the money: each issued invoice has one
PaymentIntent that the customer pays on that page by bank transfer (ACH) or
card. No Stripe Invoice or Billing object is ever created, so Stripe's
per-invoice Invoicing fee does not apply.

The full operator guide is in `plugins/invoicing/README.md`.

## Create and send an invoice

Open **Invoices**, choose **New invoice**, pick the customer, add line items, a
due date and the payment methods the customer may use, then choose **Save
draft**. **Generate payment link** assigns the next invoice number and opens the
customer's private payment page. Copy the link and send it however you like; no
email is sent for you. The customer can pay on that page or download a PDF.

## States

- **Draft**: editable, private, no Stripe object.
- **Awaiting payment**: issued, numbered, payable at the customer link.
- **Payment processing**: a bank transfer was submitted and is clearing.
- **Paid**: Stripe confirmed the full amount.
- **Paid outside Stripe**: recorded manually (check, wire).
- **Void**: cancelled; its Stripe payment is cancelled too.

Status updates arrive through the Stripe webhook and are also re-checked each
time the customer opens the page or you choose **Check payment status**.

## Review a payment or refund

Payments taken through invoices appear in **Payments & refunds**. A workspace
payment offers **Refund payment**: review the amount and reason, then approve
it. Refunds always need human approval and cannot be undone. **Recent billing
actions** keeps pending, completed and failed provider results, and **Check
provider result** reads the current status without changing the original
receipt.

## Assistant tools

The assistant can read invoices, prepare a draft, propose issuing it, check a
payment, and preview or propose a refund. Every change it prepares waits in the
approval queue until an administrator approves it.
