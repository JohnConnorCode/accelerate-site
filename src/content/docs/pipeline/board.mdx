---
title: "Use the pipeline board"
description: "Move an opportunity forward and keep its next action clear."
updated: "2026-09-08"
---

The pipeline board shows potential business by stage. Each card is an opportunity with a value, linked people, and a next action.

## 1. Open the opportunity

Open **Pipeline** and select a card. Review the contact, company, recent activity, tasks, and proposals before changing its stage.

## 2. Move it when the evidence changes

Use the card's grip to drag it to the appropriate stage. The insertion marker
shows where it will land. Release to save, or press Escape to cancel. A keyboard
user can focus the grip, press Space to lift, use arrow keys to move, and press
Space again to drop. Moving to a lost stage asks for a reason in a dialog.

On a phone, swipe the columns or use the column navigation above the board. Hold
the grip briefly to drag, or use the card's stage control to move it without
dragging. Scrolling from the card body does not start a drag. Filtered views keep
the stage control available; clear filters to reorder the complete board.

Complete any confirmation or required reason. Use the outcome of the conversation or proposal as your evidence. A stage should describe what happened, not what you hope will happen.

An AI-suggested stage change waits in [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals). Review it before authorizing the move.

## 3. Leave a next action

Make the next commitment clear: what needs to happen, who owns it, and when it is due. Overdue tasks can surface on [Today](/docs/command-center/today), where you can complete or snooze them.

## 4. Check the saved state

Wait for the saved confirmation, then confirm the intended stage and order.
If a save fails, the board checks the stored position. A stage change can succeed
before an order update fails; the message distinguishes that case. If the stored
position cannot be checked, use **Refresh board** before moving another card. If another person changed the record first, refresh and review their change before trying again. Do not overwrite a newer decision without understanding it.

## Review closed business

Won and lost deals leave the working columns. Use the Revenue view to inspect closed business, and the opportunity's history to understand how it got there. Keep related delivery work linked to the same customer records.
