A deal that has gone quiet for three weeks looks identical to a deal that is one signature away from closing, right up until someone actually opens it and checks. Pipeline at `/admin/pipeline` is the opportunity board that makes that check fast: each card is a deal carrying its stage, its value, its next action, and the people attached to it, in columns that are the live stages your workspace actually uses. Dragging a card writes a real stage change, immediately, not a suggestion.

## What actually lives here

Open opportunities, ranked for the work that moves revenue forward. Won and lost deals leave this working set once they close, because they no longer need daily attention, though the closed revenue and client value they produced stay visible in the [Revenue](/docs/pipeline/revenue) view of the same cluster.

## When the assistant suggests a move

`propose_stage_change` never writes the stage directly. It stages a proposal with the evidence it read to support it, and you approve that proposal the same way you would any other [approval](/docs/command-center/approvals). A customer reply that sounds like a yes is still just a proposal until you confirm it, because the assistant reading "sounds good, let's move forward" is not the same as you deciding that deal actually advanced.

[Using the board](/docs/pipeline/board) covers the day-to-day mechanics of moving a card yourself.

## The records behind a card

An opportunity links to contacts, companies, conversations, tasks, and proposals, and opening the card is how you see that whole graph in one place. Duplicate people do not get merged from this board, though; that review lives specifically under [Contacts](/docs/contacts), where the evidence for a match actually is.
