Billing connects what the customer agreed to buy with the invoice, recurring plan and follow-up your team manages. Use Invoices to prepare the charge, Subscriptions for supported recurring plans, and Collections to work through selected unpaid balances.

The billing navigation links Invoices, Payments & refunds, Collections, Subscriptions, Ticket sales and Revenue. Each screen keeps its own source records and available controls.

Keeping those facts distinct helps the team answer a practical question: does this account need an invoice, a reminder, a discussion about a promise, or a payment reconciliation?

## Create and send an invoice

Enable Stripe invoicing in Apps and connect the intended Stripe account. Open Invoices, choose the customer and line items, and inspect the draft before requesting its creation. Sending and publishing the customer page have their own review steps. The [invoicing guide](/docs/plugins/stripe-invoicing) explains the controls and recovery.

## Inspect payments and manage a refund

Open **Payments & refunds** from any billing screen. Filter the loaded account charges, inspect the source receipt, and export the selected records. Captured and refunded amounts stay separate by currency. **After refunds** excludes fees, disputes, payouts and bank balances.

A workspace-owned payment offers **Refund payment**. Review the amount and reason before approving it. Invoice payments with incomplete ownership information first offer **Check refund eligibility**. **Recent billing actions** preserves pending, completed and failed provider results and the original identity used for safe recovery. **Check provider result** reads the current status without changing the original receipt. See the [invoicing guide](/docs/plugins/stripe-invoicing#review-a-payment-or-refund) for the complete example and permissions.

## Offer a recurring plan

Use [Subscriptions](/docs/plugins/stripe-subscriptions) for the documented monthly and annual plans, checkout and customer account controls. Provider events establish subscription and payment state. Follow the guide to verify the account and webhook path.

## Follow up on an outstanding balance

Enable [Collections](/docs/plugins/receivables-collections), track an explicit invoice and refresh its payment evidence. Check promises, pauses and disputes before preparing a reminder. Review the recipient and wording before sending; use the case's next action to make ownership clear.

## Understand revenue separately

[Revenue](/docs/pipeline/revenue) shows current agreement and opportunity figures. Use Stripe invoice and payment facts when the question is about billed amounts or collected cash. A promise to pay records the customer's commitment and does not establish payment.

## Adapt the billing workflow

A builder can reuse the existing invoice and collection services for a custom operating screen. Keep provider balances authoritative and link domain decisions to the original customer and invoice. [Build an App](/docs/extend/apps) explains the source-development path.

## What to check

Billing needs its optional Apps and the correct provider connections. An incomplete or uncertain provider result needs the named recovery step before another attempt. Confirm the invoice or reminder result in its source workspace.
