A customer may appear in a website inquiry, a mailbox and an old spreadsheet. Contacts brings those details into linked records, so your team can find the person and follow the conversations, opportunities and work attached to them. Open **Contacts** for the canonical directory. Select **Website requests** for contact form submissions, **List import** for reviewed imports, or **Identity matching** for unfamiliar senders.

Search the directory by name or email, then open a person's history. In **Website requests**, search submissions by name, email or company. **From** and **To** set the received-date range; leave either blank for an open-ended range, or use **Reset filters** to clear the search and dates. Existing links to a submission still open that request.

## How a new row gets there

Contacts can come from supported intake, imports and connected providers. Each path has its own validation and matching rules. A CSV or pasted list uses a review batch: inspect the proposed records and confirm the exact import. [Import contacts](/docs/contacts/import) walks through that process.

## How two records become one

Use record identifiers and source evidence to establish which person a conversation belongs to. **Identity matching** offers suggested matches for an unfamiliar sender and lets you choose the right contact, create one when appropriate or leave the item for later. A similar name alone is insufficient evidence for a match. Keep unresolved records available for review rather than merging their histories by assumption.

## Following the graph

Open the linked company, opportunity, conversation or task to understand the relationship behind a contact. These links help the team continue work with its source context. A recorded relationship is evidence to inspect; it does not establish permission to request or make an introduction.

The contact relationship timeline shows each interaction in a separate card with its type, date and source link. Select a card, or focus it with Tab and press Enter, to continue in the linked conversation, opportunity or task. Client records use the same timeline.

## Review an unfamiliar sender

Open **Identity matching** from Contacts when a conversation sender needs a match. Choose a suggested contact and select **Match selected contact**, or enter a full name and choose **Create contact**. For example, if Jordan Mitchell emails Northline Roofing from a new address, compare the suggested contacts before linking the conversation. **No match** records your decision without linking anyone; **Review later** keeps the item waiting.

When the list is empty, **No contacts need review** means there are no items waiting in this queue. If the list cannot load, use **Retry**; an unavailable list cannot tell you whether reviews are waiting.

If a contact relationship cannot load, the page shows a load error with **Retry**. A failed request does not mean the person has no history. Retrying reloads the same record; switching people never displays the previous person’s relationship as a placeholder.
