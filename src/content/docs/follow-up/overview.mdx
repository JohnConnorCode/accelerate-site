A customer question can remain unanswered when nobody owns the response. Use this workflow to read the request, assign a clear next action and confirm the result, keeping the conversation and commitment visible to the team.

## Record the next action

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Open the inquiry">
    Find it in [Leads and chat](/docs/sources/leads), or its thread in
    [Conversations](/docs/conversations).
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Read what's already happened">
    Read the latest message and check whether anyone has already replied, so you're not duplicating
    a response.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Assign it, with a date">
    Assign responsibility for the response and record a due date on the relevant task or
    opportunity.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Write a real next action">
    "Send the revised estimate to Lee by Thursday" tells the next person exactly what to do. "Follow
    up" does not.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

## Respond and verify

Prepare the reply using the customer's actual request and the business records linked to them, checking recipients, commitments, prices, and dates before you send it. [Replying](/docs/conversations/reply) covers the send itself; check the resulting status afterward to confirm it went.

If the full answer will take longer, tell the customer what you're checking and roughly when you'll respond, and record that commitment so it shows up in the team's own work queue rather than only in your head.

## Reviewing what's still open

Use [Today](/docs/command-center/today) to see due tasks and waiting decisions across the team. After a reply or a meeting, update the next action: close the task once the promised work is done, or record a new date and reason when it changes.

## What to check

- If delivery is uncertain, inspect the [action result](/docs/start/receipts) before sending anything again.
- If the person has declined or asked to stop, record that decision and apply your workspace's stop controls before any further outreach.
