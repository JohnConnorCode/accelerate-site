title: "Follow up on an inquiry"
description: "Assign the response, record a due date and close the loop."
updated: "2026-09-05"
---

Use this workflow when a person has asked a question, requested an estimate or left a conversation waiting for your response.

## Record the next action

1. Open the inquiry in [Leads and chat](/docs/sources/leads) or its thread in [Conversations](/docs/conversations).
2. Read the latest message and check whether anyone has already replied.
3. Assign responsibility for the response and record a due date on the relevant task or opportunity.
4. Write a next action someone else could carry out: “Send the revised estimate to Lee by Thursday” gives more direction than “Follow up”.

## Respond and verify

Prepare the reply using the customer's actual request and the linked business records. Check recipients, commitments, prices and dates before sending. Follow [Replying](/docs/conversations/reply) and inspect the resulting status.

If the full answer will take longer, tell the customer what you are checking and when you will respond. Record that commitment so it appears in the team's work queue.

## Review outstanding work

Use [Today](/docs/command-center/today) to review due tasks and waiting decisions. After a reply or meeting, update the next action. Close the task when the promised work is complete, or record a new date and reason when it changes.

If delivery is uncertain, inspect the [action result](/docs/start/receipts) before sending again. If the person has declined or asked to stop, record that decision and apply the workspace's stop controls before further outreach.
