---
title: "Follow through on every inquiry"
description: "Response time, steady follow-up, and a clear next step on every page."
updated: "2026-09-04"
---

Slow first responses, estimates that go quiet, and follow-up that depends on memory can leave inquiries unresolved. Assign an owner and a clear next step to keep each conversation moving.

## Respond first

Measure response time in minutes, assign every inquiry an owner, and confirm receipt right away even when the full answer takes longer.

## Follow through

An inquiry that waits for you while you serve customers is an inquiry at risk. Give every open thread a next action with a date, and review the list daily until each one is booked, closed, or released.

## Make the next step obvious

Every page should offer one clear action: call, book a conversation, or request a quote. Make that action easy to find and explain what happens next.
