---
title: "Never lose an inquiry"
description: "Response time, steady follow-up, and a clear next step on every page."
updated: "2026-09-04"
---

Most lost inquiries die from slow first response, estimates that go quiet, and follow-up that depends on somebody remembering.

## Respond first

The business that responds first usually keeps the conversation. Measure response time in minutes, assign every inquiry an owner, and confirm receipt right away even when the full answer takes longer.

## Follow through

An inquiry that waits for you while you serve customers is an inquiry at risk. Give every open thread a next action with a date, and review the list daily until each one is booked, closed, or released.

## Make the next step obvious

Every page should offer one clear action: call, book a conversation, or request a quote. If a visitor has to figure out what to do next, most of them will not do it.
