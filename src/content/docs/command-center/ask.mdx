When a customer needs a reply or a client handoff needs work, tell Ask AI the result you want. It can read the relevant records, prepare changes and show their results in the same conversation. Open it from Today or the **AI Workspace**, then name the customer or record.

## Ask something specific

A clear target and a clear result you need beats a vague prompt every time. Some real shapes that work well:

- "What needs my attention today?"
- "Show the recent timeline for this contact."
- "What is the next action on this opportunity?"
- "Draft a follow-up for this conversation, using the latest reply."

Choose the relevant record when the interface offers you that context, and resolve an ambiguous person or company before you ask for a change, so the assistant isn't guessing which one you meant either.

For recurring billing, Ask AI can explain only the current workspace records and registered capabilities. Stripe remains the payment authority. Use the customer account for checkout, invoices, payment details, cancellation, and plan changes; the assistant cannot charge or change a subscription unless a registered tool explicitly offers that action.

## Checking the evidence

Read the tool results or record links behind an answer before you act on it. If the answer is partial, look at what was unavailable rather than treating a gap as a fact the assistant confirmed; a disconnected provider or an unavailable tool can limit what it's able to answer, and the honest response to that is telling you it doesn't know, not guessing.

## Reviewing anything it proposes

A request can produce exact proposals. Choose **Review** beneath the answer to inspect the recipient, wording or record changes inside the conversation. Approve or reject each change there. After opening one exact review, you can also type “approve” or “reject”; those words are handled as your decision, never as an agent tool.

Approval records your decision. The result records what happened, including a failed or partial action. Returning to the conversation restores pending proposals and their recorded statuses. [Work → Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) remains available for the full action receipt. If a run stops early, inspect its proposals before repeating it.

## Delegate an ordered job

Ask for a plan such as “Read this client's records, prepare the handoff tasks, then draft a follow-up.” The agent previews up to eight ordered steps before starting saved work. It can continue after you close the browser when the workspace scheduler and a finite AI call budget are configured.

Use **Read progress** in chat to retrieve saved steps and receipts. Pause stops future work, Resume continues eligible steps, and Cancel stops future steps while retaining completed changes. A step awaiting an action decision cannot advance until the required actions have finished. Interrupted steps need reconciliation rather than being repeated automatically.

## Allow bounded internal work

You can ask the agent to prepare permission for a specific internal operation on named records. The exact review includes allowed fields, an expiry within 30 days and a daily action limit. You must approve the permission yourself.

Eligible operations cover tasks, notes, contact tags, opportunity next actions and open pipeline stages. Current membership, policy, record scope, permitted fields, expiry and remaining daily allowance are checked before execution. Messages, publishing, billing, deletion and permission changes still require human approval. If permission does not cover a proposed change, it stays pending for review.
