A proposal looks different than it did last week, and nobody on your team remembers changing it. Activity at `/admin/activity` is where you go to answer that question with evidence instead of a guess: who changed a record, what action was recorded, and exactly when it happened.

## Finding a change

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Narrow the time range">
    Set the window to roughly when you think the event occurred; a tight range surfaces the right
    event faster than scrolling a long list.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Filter it down">
    Filter by actor, action, source, or entity type where those filters are available for what
    you're looking for.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Open the linked record">
    Find the matching event and open the record it points to, rather than trusting the event summary
    alone.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Compare against current state">
    Check the recorded action against the record's current state and any related execution or
    provider receipt to see whether they actually agree.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

For example, if a proposal appears to have changed right after a customer reviewed it, start with that proposal's own events around the time of the review, and keep the proposal's identifier with your findings so a colleague can pull up the exact same record later.

## Reading the evidence correctly

An activity event records that something happened inside the application. For anything with an external effect, like a message going out, check the associated result too: an approved request, provider acceptance, and final delivery are three different facts, and the event log alone only tells you the first one happened. [Action results](/docs/start/receipts) walks through the rest.

Events deliberately omit sensitive content, so a row can identify that a message was sent without containing its full body. Open the authorized business record itself for content you're allowed to read.

## What to check

- If you can't find the event you're looking for, double-check the workspace, date range, and filters before assuming it's missing. If it's genuinely absent, keep the affected record's ID and approximate time to hand to your technical owner rather than guessing at what happened.
- [Today](/docs/command-center/today) shows you work to act on; Activity is for investigating what was already recorded, not for finding new work.
