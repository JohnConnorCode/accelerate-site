Activity at `/admin/activity` is the operator view of the audit ledger. It records material changes: actor, action, entity, source, and a before/after snapshot. It is how you answer "who did this?" without reading a database.

## What is stored

Each row is one change. Filters cover actor, entity type, action, source, and a date range. Opening a row with a known entity takes you to that record: an opportunity, a conversation, a task on Today, a proposal, or a campaign.

Prompt text, raw customer bodies, tokens, and secrets do not belong here. If a field would leak those, the service redacts it or omits it.

## What it is for

Use Activity when a send, a stage move, or an approval needs a receipt you can show someone. Provider facts stay immutable. Human notes and configuration are edited through their own services, and those edits still write a row.

System exceptions on [Today](/docs/command-center/today) (a missed health cadence, a webhook that keeps failing) are the same facts Setup Center uses. Activity is the history; Today is the queue.
