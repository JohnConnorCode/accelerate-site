Every business has a version of the same problem: the most urgent thing to do this morning is buried under a dozen less urgent ones, and finding it means scanning an inbox, a task list, and a calendar separately before you can even start. Today does that scanning for you. Open it at `/admin/today` at the start of your session, and you get replies, due tasks, upcoming meetings, proposals, approvals, and system problems in one ranked queue.

## The four sections, and what each one actually is

**Needs your decision** holds pending approvals with an exact change to review, the only section where something waits specifically on your yes or no. **Your work** is tasks, including recovery follow-ups, with completion and snooze controls right there. **Watch** surfaces conversations, proposals, and operational signals worth investigating, though you go to their source screen to actually act on them. **Upcoming** is meetings and dated context worth preparing for before they happen rather than after.

These four sections are views over the same underlying records, not a separate to-do list, so opening a signal here does not create a new task somewhere else, and a custom App can still keep its own native work type and interface if it needs one. [Work](/docs/command-center/work) gives you the same tasks and approvals with ownership and status filters for editing; both screens share the same saved state, so a change in one shows up in the other immediately.

## Working one item

<StepByStep>
<Step number={1} title="Read the reason">
Check the item's stated reason and due date, then open its linked record for the latest context before you act on it.
</Step>
<Step number={2} title="Take the offered action">
Respond, review an approval, complete a task, or snooze to a later date where that option exists.
</Step>
<Step number={3} title="Check content before approving a send">
For an approval, inspect the exact proposed change. For a message specifically, read the recipients and the full body, not just that a draft exists.
</Step>
<Step number={4} title="Confirm the result">
Refresh the queue if needed, and check that the item is actually resolved or has a specific next step, rather than assuming your action was the last one required.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

Completing a task records that the task itself is done; it says nothing about whether an email was delivered or a customer accepted a proposal, so check the [action result](/docs/start/receipts) whenever the work touched an external service.

## Deciding what to do first

Weigh urgency, deadline, and the stated reason together rather than picking whatever sits at the top. A customer waiting on a response you already promised often deserves attention before an undated internal task, even if the task looks older. If you postpone something, leave a date and enough context that the next person, which might be you tomorrow, can pick it up without re-investigating from scratch.

Ask AI can summarize the records behind an item for you, and it is worth using for exactly that. Review the evidence it shows you before acting on its recommendation, the same way you would with a colleague's summary.

## What to check

- If an item is still there after you act on it, inspect the saved record and refresh before assuming something is broken. Never repeat an external action, like resending an email, just to make a stubborn row disappear.
- If a refresh fails, the page may be showing you its last successful load rather than current state, so check that load's age before you rely on it.
- An empty queue means nothing is currently ranked as urgent, not that every connected system is healthy. Use [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) for connection problems and [Activity](/docs/command-center/activity) to inspect what actually happened.
