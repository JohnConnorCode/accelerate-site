Open `/admin/today` at the start of your work session. Today gathers items that need attention and names the kind of attention required: **Do**, **Approve**, **Decide**, or **Consider**.

Today is a view, not a second work record. A task or approval can appear here and in [Tasks and approvals](/docs/command-center/work); completing or resolving it in either place changes the same source record.

## Work one item

1. Read the item's reason and due date. Open its linked record to check the latest context.
2. Take the offered action: respond, review an approval, complete a task or choose a later date where snooze is available.
3. For an approval, inspect the exact proposed change. For a message, check the recipients and full body before approving a send.
4. Check the result. Refresh the queue if needed and confirm the item is resolved or has a specific next step.

Completing a task records that the task is done. It does not prove an email was delivered or a customer accepted a proposal. Check the relevant [action result](/docs/start/receipts) when work involves an external service.

## Read the four states

- **Do** means a person needs to complete work.
- **Approve** means a proposed change or external action is waiting for your decision.
- **Decide** means a customer, deal, or identity question needs a human choice.
- **Consider** means a signal or upcoming moment deserves context; it is not automatically a task.

Proposal follow-up and meeting preparation are **Do** items. An unresolved contact match is a **Decide** item. Optional opportunity signals are **Consider** items until you turn one into work.

## Decide what to do first

Use urgency, deadline and the stated reason together. A customer waiting for a promised response may need attention before an undated internal task. If you postpone work, leave a date and enough context for the next person to continue.

Ask Accelerate can help summarize the underlying records. Review the evidence before accepting its recommendation.

## When the queue looks wrong

- **An item remains after you act:** inspect the saved record and refresh. Do not repeat an external action just to clear the row.
- **A refresh fails:** the page may retain the last successful queue. Check its age before relying on it.
- **Nothing appears:** confirm the workspace and loading state. An empty queue is not proof that every connected system is healthy.

Use [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) for connection problems and [Activity](/docs/command-center/activity) to inspect recorded changes.
