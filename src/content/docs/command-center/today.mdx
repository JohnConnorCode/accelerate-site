Some mornings need a quick decision. Others need space to work out what comes next. Today brings your business context and next actions together without requiring a full pipeline or a busy inbox to be useful. Open Today, read the snapshot, then choose what deserves your involvement.

## Make the page fit your day

Choose **Customize** to rename the current view, adjust spacing, add or remove modules, and arrange them by dragging or using the arrow buttons. **Preview arrangement** shows the module widths before you save. On a wide desktop, primary and supporting modules stack independently so a long list does not leave a gap below a shorter one. Full-width modules separate those groups. On narrower screens, all modules follow your saved order in one column.

Each module has a width and item limit. Attention modules also support type, source, time-horizon and task-owner filters. Save for **Just me** to keep your arrangement private, or **Everyone in this workspace** to change a shared view. The current workspace administrator role can manage shared views. Check **Open this view by default** to use it on your next visit.

For example, duplicate Business overview, name the copy “Focused day,” move Needs you first, set its time horizon to Through today, and save it for Just me as your default. Reload Today: Focused day remains selected. Your colleagues' personal defaults and the original shared arrangement are unchanged. Cancel closes an unsaved draft without changing the saved view.

Open **More** beside Customize to create, duplicate or delete a view. None of those controls deletes business records. You can save up to 12 views per scope, with up to 16 module instances in each view.

## Read the context, then act

The **Business snapshot** links decisions to approvals, follow-up to Work, and opportunity counts and value to Pipeline. When a completed Business Pulse brief is available, its AI interpretations are labeled separately and link to their supporting facts. An interpretation is omitted when its evidence changes or it is more than a day old.

**Needs you** holds decisions and commitments. Open an item to inspect its reason, next step and related context. Complete or reschedule a task there, review an approval's exact change, or choose **Prepare next step** to open Ask AI with the source identified. Preparing a prompt does not execute it or send a message.

Pin an item to put it first within matching modules. Mute a watch finding to hide that exact evidence from your personal view; changed evidence brings it back. **Restore muted findings** clears those preferences. Choose **More → Show all attention** to include items excluded by the current arrangement or personal filters.

**Being handled** shows recent coworker work, its recorded status, available outcomes and scheduled checks. **Business changes** and **Upcoming** separate watch signals from dated commitments. Add **Pipeline snapshot** or **Recent activity** when you want an additional pipeline summary or the recorded timeline alongside your work.

These are views over existing records. [Work](/docs/command-center/work) remains the editing workspace for tasks and approvals, and [Activity](/docs/command-center/activity) records what actually happened.

## Bring App follow-up into the day

**App follow-up** shows bounded records from enabled Collections and Opportunity Radar Apps. Open an entry to continue in its native workspace. The App still owns its records, permissions and approval workflow. Disabling it removes its Today contribution without deleting its records.

For example, a Northline billing case appears with its next action. Open it in Collections to inspect outstanding invoice evidence before preparing a reminder. Opening Today does not contact the billing provider, send a reminder or run a Radar model request.

## When there is little to show

Empty work modules explain what is missing and offer the relevant next step. Missing snapshot data appears as unavailable, not as a healthy zero. Short modules take only the space they need; a longer neighboring list does not push the next primary module down.

The page refreshes approximately every minute while active and when you return to the window. It holds the current content while you interact with a module or review a dialog. **Show updates** lets you apply new context deliberately. Your saved layout never rearranges itself because the record count changed.

## What to check

- A failed source reports its own unavailable state while other modules remain usable. On a failed refresh, retained content is the last successful observation, not proof that a task or connection is current.
- If another session saves first, reload your views and review the newer arrangement before saving again. An uncertain save can be retried with the same changes without creating a second receipt.
- Ask AI can read views and prepare exact layout or preference changes for approval. Only the proposing member can inspect the private preview and approve it. Changes still require current workspace access and the expected saved revision.
- Factual context does not need a model request. Synthesized briefs use the existing Business Pulse worker, configured AI connection and budgets; unavailable AI falls back to facts. Viewing Today never starts a paid model call.
- **Use classic Today** returns the workspace to its earlier page. An administrator can enable the new arrangement again without discarding saved views.
- Self-hosted installations need the Today workspace migration and schema verification before saved views can be used. Missing storage preserves the existing preferences and reports an error; it does not silently replace them.
