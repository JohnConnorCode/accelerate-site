Command Center starts with the business facts you already have. It adds attention and automation around those facts without making a second copy of the record.

## The shared loop

1. **Record or event** — a message, meeting, invoice, opportunity, or customer update changes.
2. **Signal or rule** — the system notices something that may matter, such as a stalled deal or overdue balance.
3. **Workflow** — background logic evaluates what should happen.
4. **Human attention** — a Task, Approval, or domain-native work type appears only when a person must act.
5. **Action** — a command sends, schedules, updates, or creates something under its policy.
6. **Activity** — the result is recorded with its actor, source, and receipt.

**Today is a view**, not another object. It groups the same canonical items into **Do**, **Approve**, **Decide**, and **Consider** so an owner can choose what to do next. Successful background workflows stay out of Today; exceptions become a clear signal or work item when a person is needed.
