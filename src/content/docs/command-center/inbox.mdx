A new inquiry arrives while your team is busy with existing customers. **Intake review** at `/admin/inbox` puts incoming items and follow-ups in one queue, so someone can find the source and take the next step before it is forgotten.

## Work through the queue

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Find the item">
    Start with **All**, or choose the screen's **Leads**, **Contacts**, **Chat**, **Tasks**,
    **Proposals**, **Partners**, **Coworkers**, or **Actions** filter. Use **Search the queue** to
    narrow the visible items by name or text.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Open its source">
    Select a row to open its record. Read the customer context and decide the follow-up there;
    Intake review gathers the items, while the owning screen holds the full details.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Finish or review">
    You can complete a task or mark a contact reviewed from its row. Choosing **Approve** on an
    action opens its exact review in Work, where you inspect the target and proposed change before
    deciding. Other items continue in their own record.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Check the result">
    The completed task or reviewed contact leaves this queue. For a proposed action, check
    [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) and its recorded result before repeating it.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

For example, Northline Roofing can open a new website inquiry, read the linked contact and conversation, then reply from Conversations. If the team records a follow-up task, it remains visible in [Work](/docs/command-center/work) until it is completed.

Coworker work appears when its creator saved an Inbox task bridge. The queue shows that work once rather than repeating its bridge as a separate task. Pending actions use their recorded title, explanation and urgency, and open the exact approval in Work.

## Choose the right starting place

[Today](/docs/command-center/today) ranks the decisions and commitments that need attention now. Intake review groups incoming items by type, including work that may not be urgent yet. Use [Conversations](/docs/conversations/reply) when you are ready to read a thread and send a reply.

## What to check

- If the list looks empty, clear the selected filter and search before concluding that the queue is clear.
- Choose **Refresh** for a new read. If it fails, the last loaded list may remain on screen; use the shown error and retry before treating its counts as current.
- A failed source read makes the queue unavailable; an empty list is not substituted for missing evidence.
- An action's **Approve** control takes you to Work for exact review. Confirm the recipient, content and result there rather than assuming the row click executed it.
