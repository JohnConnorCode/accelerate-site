A website inquiry, a contact import and a chat handoff each need someone to decide what happens next. **Inbox** brings review work together so you can inspect the source, resolve its context and choose the appropriate follow-up.

## Filtering to one kind of thing

The default view, All, shows everything at once, with live counts that update as new items arrive. You can narrow it to just leads, contacts, chat handoffs, tasks, proposals, partners, coworker output, or staged actions when you want to work through one category without the rest competing for your attention.

## Working a row

Opening a row takes you to the actual record it belongs to, where you reply, accept, reject, or assign it using the same services the rest of the workspace already runs on. Inbox itself is a queue over those records, not a separate system with its own rules, so nothing you do here behaves differently than doing the equivalent action from the record's own screen.

Refresh reloads the list without navigating you away from the page, and if that reload fails, the last good list you saw stays visible rather than disappearing, so a flaky connection does not leave you staring at an empty screen.

## How this differs from Today and Conversations

Today ranks the most urgent subset of work across your whole workspace, including some of what shows up here, but organized by urgency rather than by where the item entered. Conversations is where you write and send a reply to a thread. Inbox is the catch-all for anything new that has not been worked yet, regardless of urgency. A reasonable rhythm is Today to start your morning, Inbox to clear whatever is new, and Conversations whenever you are specifically answering someone.
