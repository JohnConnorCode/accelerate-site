Use this catalog to find an area of functionality. Your workspace's modules, provider connections, and policies determine which parts are available to you.

**This is a product reference, not a live readiness report.** Check [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) and [Integrations](/docs/workspace/integrations) for the state of your own installation. Availability and approval policy can differ by workspace.

Entries marked **Review workflow** describe capabilities involving proposed actions. See [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) for the difference between an AI proposal, a manual action, and an approved operation.
