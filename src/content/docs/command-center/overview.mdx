## Follow a piece of work

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Start with Today">
    Open [Today](/docs/command-center/today) to see Sales, Customer follow-up, Delivery and Money in
    Business review. Open an item to learn why it needs attention and which record it came from.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Read the source record">
    Use [Contacts](/docs/contacts) for the customer history, [Conversations](/docs/conversations)
    for the thread, or [Pipeline](/docs/pipeline) for the opportunity and its next action.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Move the next step forward">
    Use [Work](/docs/command-center/work) to edit, snooze or complete an assigned task. If Ask AI
    prepares a change, inspect its exact target and content in
    [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) before deciding.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Check what happened">
    Open [Activity](/docs/command-center/activity) and the affected record. A saved task, an
    approved request, and a message delivered by a provider are different results.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

For example, Northline Roofing can open a proposal follow-up from Today, check the customer's latest reply, and set the existing task's due date in Work. The task stays with the customer context, so a teammate can understand the work without reconstructing the story.

## Find the workspace for the job

The sidebar has seven business groups. **Daily work** brings together Today, tasks, approvals and recent results. **Customers & sales** contains the customer history, conversations, opportunities and proposals. **Delivery** organizes client work and commitments. **Billing** covers invoices, recurring plans, collections and revenue.

Use **Marketing** for website pages, forms, campaigns and editorial work. **Apps & AI** contains the AI workspace and specialized capabilities you can adapt. **Workspace** holds settings, connections and access. Expand a group to see its related screens. Search uses the same destination names, and previous names such as Site Studio still find **Website & pages**.

**Start here** explains the first useful action on a screen. **How this works** expands the steps and saved-result explanation, with a guide for deeper context. [Follow one customer from inquiry to billing](/docs/start/daily-path), or choose a workflow in **Getting started** when you want to practice a specific job.

## Choose the next guide

Open **Search**, or press **Cmd/Ctrl+K**, to find tasks, opportunities, clients, proposals, people, pages and available commands. Enter at least three characters to search workspace records. Results are grouped by record type and include status or relationship context; select one to open its detail view. Use the arrow keys and Enter to navigate without leaving the search field. Clients and proposals appear only when their modules are enabled. If a record read fails, choose **Retry search**; pages and local commands remain available while it recovers. A failed read shows an error instead of claiming there are no matches.

[Intake review](/docs/command-center/inbox) groups incoming items by type when you need to clear new work. [Ask AI](/docs/command-center/ask) helps with a question across the records your workspace can access. Saved Today views let a person or administrator arrange the daily page, while Work offers personal and shared task views for editing.

Optional [Apps and plugins](/docs/plugins) add specialized workflows while keeping their own records and approval rules. For example, a configured [subscription workflow](/docs/plugins/stripe-subscriptions) lets a customer manage a recurring plan, while Work still shows the team's internal follow-up.

Start with the [fictional demo path](/docs/start/daily-path) if you want to practice without connecting an account. For a connected installation, use [Setup](/docs/command-center/setup) to verify the sources your workflow needs. The [capability reference](/docs/command-center/capabilities) describes supported work, and the [extension guides](/docs/extend) show how builders can add their own screens and tools.

Use [Work](/docs/command-center/work) to add instructions to a task and return to its related client, contact or opportunity. The task inspector keeps its own page link and recovery controls, so a failed read or save can be handled where the work is open.

## What to check

- Check [Setup](/docs/command-center/setup) before relying on connected email, calendar, AI or payment data. A listed capability may still need configuration in your workspace.
- Confirm the affected record and action result after a decision. A prepared draft or approved proposal does not by itself confirm delivery to a customer.
- Ask AI can propose supported changes, while manual controls use their own confirmation and access rules. The [approvals guide](/docs/command-center/approvals) explains how eligible actions can earn a proposed increase in autonomy.
