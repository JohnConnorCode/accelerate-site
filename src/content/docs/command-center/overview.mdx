Command Center connects the things that otherwise get separated: a customer's message, the deal it belongs to, what you promised, and the next action. Start your day with the work that needs attention and open the supporting records from there.

## Start with a complete workflow

[Try your first workflow](/docs/start/daily-path) in the fictional demo. You will review a proposed action and check its result without connecting any accounts.

## Where to do your work

| You want to…                | Open                                        | What you find                                             |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Decide what needs attention | [Today](/docs/command-center/today)         | Ranked work with a reason and next action                 |
| Triage new intake           | [Inbox](/docs/command-center/inbox)         | Items that still need a decision or owner                 |
| Answer a customer           | [Conversations](/docs/conversations/reply)  | The thread and reply composer                             |
| Move a deal forward         | [Pipeline](/docs/pipeline/board)            | Stages, deal context, and next actions                    |
| Find a person or company    | [Contacts](/docs/contacts)                  | Shared records and their linked history                   |
| Review an AI proposal       | [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) | The proposed effect before it runs                        |
| Check what happened         | [Activity](/docs/command-center/activity)   | Recorded changes and their origin                         |
| Ask about your business     | [Ask AI](/docs/command-center/ask)          | Answers grounded in the tools available to your workspace |

## How AI fits

The assistant reads available context and can prepare a proposal. You review the proposed action through the same workspace controls. A manual action, such as sending a reply you wrote, follows that screen's own confirmation flow. [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) explains the distinction.

After an action runs, check its result. Prepared, approved, sent, and delivered are different states; a draft is not a sent message.

## What is available in your workspace

Modules and provider connections determine which features you can use. A capability listed in the [reference](/docs/command-center/capabilities) is not proof that your workspace has connected and verified its provider. Check [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) before relying on a live workflow.

For installation, see [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting). For customization and external assistants, see [Build on it](/docs/extend).
