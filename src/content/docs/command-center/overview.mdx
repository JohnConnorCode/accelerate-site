Command Center keeps those records connected, so you can understand the situation and decide what should happen next. Start with Today for priorities, then open the source record to do the work.

## Where the work happens

Today also supports saved personal and shared arrangements. Combine a sourced business
brief, decisions, commitments, coworker progress and enabled App follow-up, then
adjust the layout to fit a focused day or a broad review. Sparse and empty views
offer a starting point without inventing records. The [Today guide](/docs/command-center/today)
walks through saving an arrangement and acting from its source context.

**[Today](/docs/command-center/today)** is where your day starts: replies waiting on you, tasks due, and proposals ready for a decision, ranked with the reason each one is there. **[Inbox](/docs/command-center/inbox)** is what to triage before it becomes urgent, new inquiries and messages that still need an owner. When you are ready to answer someone, **[Conversations](/docs/conversations/reply)** has the thread and a composer that already knows who you are replying to and what deal they are attached to.

Moving a deal forward happens on the **[Pipeline](/docs/pipeline/board)** board, where every card carries its stage, its value, and the people connected to it. **[Contacts](/docs/contacts)** is where you find a person or company and see everything linked to them in one place. When your assistant has prepared something, **[Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals)** is where you decide whether it runs, and **[Activity](/docs/command-center/activity)** is where you go afterward to confirm it happened. **[Ask AI](/docs/command-center/ask)** answers a specific question using whatever the workspace can see, and shows you the records it used to answer it. **[Work](/docs/command-center/work)** is the filtered task-and-approval list underneath all of it, the same records as Today, organized for editing rather than triage.

Recurring revenue sits alongside that operating work. With Stripe subscriptions enabled, admins create plans in **[Subscriptions](/docs/plugins/stripe-subscriptions)** and customers use a branded Plans and account flow to check out, manage payment details, and understand renewal status. The account keeps asynchronous Stripe confirmation and payment attention visible instead of presenting a missing record as success.

## Review the next action

The assistant can read connected context and prepare a proposed change, such as a reply, stage move or task. Open the proposal to inspect its exact target and content before approving it. Approval authorizes execution; the resulting receipt tells you what happened.

Some internal actions can become eligible for less frequent review under an `ask_until_trusted` policy. The graduation scan looks for at least three approvals over seven days and checks the action's eligibility. You still decide whether to change its policy. External sends, campaign activation and invoice actions retain their required human review. [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) explains the current rules.

Manual controls use their screen's confirmation and authorization rules. Check the saved result after either path: a prepared draft, an approved request, a provider-accepted send and confirmed delivery are distinct states.

## What your workspace can do

What you see depends on which modules are turned on and which providers you have connected and verified. Seeing a capability listed in the [reference](/docs/command-center/capabilities) tells you the product supports it; it does not tell you your workspace has connected that provider yet. [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) is where to check before you promise a live workflow to your team.
