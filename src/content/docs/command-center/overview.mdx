After [installation](/docs/self-hosting), sign in at `/admin`. The Command Center is the authenticated operator shell. Navigation labels below match `src/lib/admin/navigation.ts`. A fictional walkthrough lives at `/demo/command-center` and does not write to your workspace.

## Today

**Today** (`/admin/today`) is the ranked queue. Each row has a reason and a recommended next action. Tasks from inbound capture show **Complete task** and **Snooze until tomorrow**. Proposed actions show **Review before approving**. If the live read fails, the page title stays **Today** and the subtitle is **The founder queue could not be assembled yet.** Retry the read, or open Setup Center. No work is hidden by that failure.

## Pipeline

**Pipeline** (`/admin/pipeline`) is the opportunity board. A captured lead lands in the **New** column on the default seed (`new`). Open a card to see the contact, company, next action, **Verified activity**, and **Related work**. Stage moves go through the pipeline service; leaving a won or lost stage requires a reason.

## Inbox

**Inbox** (`/admin/inbox`) is unowned or untriaged work: leads, contacts, chat handoffs, tasks, proposals, partners, coworker items, and actions. Use it when Today is filtered or a task is easy to miss.

## Setup Center and Integrations

**Setup Center** (`/admin/setup`) reports connection and readiness. Status values are ready, action, degraded, optional, and disabled. Configuration is not delivery health. **Integrations** (`/admin/integrations`) lists capabilities, evidence, and planned providers that remain unavailable until their card ships.

## Activity

**Activity** (`/admin/activity`) is the audit history: actor, origin, target, and before/after for material changes. Opportunity-level **Verified activity** on the record page is the canonical activity ledger, including capture receipts such as **Manual lead captured**.

## Approvals versus work

Work is a task you complete or snooze. An approval is a queued action that must be confirmed before it executes. [Workspace, record, work, approval, and receipt](/docs/start) defines those words. [Your first result](/docs/start/first-value) walks one inquiry through both the task and the receipt.
