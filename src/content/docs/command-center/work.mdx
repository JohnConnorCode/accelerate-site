Today is built for triage: skim the queue, act on what matters most, move on. Work, at `/admin/work`, is built for the opposite: sit with your whole task list, filter it down, and edit anything that needs a correction rather than just acting on it. Both screens read the exact same records, so a change here shows up on Today immediately and vice versa.

## Finding and editing a task

<StepByStep>
<Step number={1} title="Choose an ownership view">
Open **Tasks**, then pick **Team work**, **My work**, or **Unassigned**, and select an open, snoozed, or completed status to match what you're looking for.
</Step>
<Step number={2} title="Narrow the list">
Filter by source or search the task title and related record name. The list shows up to 100 tasks for whichever ownership and status you selected.
</Step>
<Step number={3} title="Open and edit">
Select a task to open its inspector, change its title, due date, or priority, and choose **Save changes**.
</Step>
<Step number={4} title="Confirm it stuck">
Return to Today and check the task where it's due. An edit keeps the task's existing status; it does not mark the task complete on its own.
</Step>
</StepByStep>

Use the completion control specifically when work is actually finished. Today also offers snooze until tomorrow, and a snoozed task stays visible in Work's **Snoozed** view rather than disappearing, so nothing gets lost by deferring it. Completing a snoozed task clears the snooze and records completion against that same original task, not a new one.

The ownership filter is a lens for browsing, not an assignment tool: changing whose work you're looking at does not reassign anything. Assignment follows whatever workflow originally created the task, and a custom App can keep its own native queue and lifecycle entirely separate from this one.

## Reviewing a decision

Open **Approvals**, select a proposal, and inspect its exact content, target, and consequence before deciding. Today and Work share the same review inspector and execution service underneath, so a decision you make here behaves identically to making it from Today. Once you decide, that pending approval clears from both views at once; it never creates a second record of the same decision.

Read [approval results](/docs/command-center/approvals) before you interpret what a provider action actually did. Approval, execution, and delivery are three separate facts, and only the last one tells you a customer actually received something.

## Checking the saved result

[Activity](/docs/command-center/activity) keeps the recorded history of what changed here. A completed task confirms the task is done, and nothing more: it does not confirm a payment arrived, an email sent, or a customer accepted a proposal, so open the relevant record for those results specifically.

## What to check

- If saving fails, the inspector keeps whatever you typed and shows the error rather than discarding your edit; refresh the record before retrying if the operation might be stale.
- An empty filtered list usually means your ownership or status filter is narrower than you meant, not that the workspace genuinely has no work.
- The fictional demo uses these same screens with isolated session data. Edits, snoozes, and completions persist for that session and reset when the demo workspace resets.
