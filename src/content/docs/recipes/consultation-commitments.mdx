A consultation ends with documents to request and a follow-up to arrange. Record the agreed administrative work against the meeting so responsibilities remain visible.

This recipe uses existing capabilities in sequence, so your team can keep the source context while assigning the work that follows. Start with one fictional example and check its saved result before adapting it to your operation.

<RecipeIngredients id="consultation-commitments" />

## Before you start

Use an authorized workspace with readable calendar events and the Meeting preparation and Meeting commitments plugins enabled. Both plugins start disabled. Confirm the meeting exists in the workspace and choose valid task assignees.

Explore the [law firms workspace](/demo/command-center/alder-ridge-law/today) with fictional records. Use it to inspect the shared screens and simulated actions; configure real forms or provider connections in a workspace you control.

## Complete the workflow

1. Run **Meeting preparation** from Plugins to find upcoming, non-cancelled meetings in its 48-hour window. Open the source meeting and review the customer context.

2. After **consultation follow-up**, agree the next steps with the team. For this example: **Request the agreed documents through the firm’s approved channel; confirm the next appointment**.

3. Open **Meeting commitments**, select the stored meeting and enter the agreed tasks with descriptions, due dates and valid assignees. A plan supports one to ten tasks.

4. Preview and submit the checklist for approval. Approve the exact plan through the shared action queue after reviewing its source, dates and owners.

5. Inspect the execution result and open the created tasks in **Work**. Complete or update them as the commitments are fulfilled.

## Check the result

Assigned administrative commitments linked to the consultation. Each task has an owner and date, and the source meeting remains available when someone needs to understand the commitment.

## Adapt it for your business

A matter-management App can add its own records and deadlines while reusing contacts and permissions.

Give a developer or coding assistant the business outcome, the records it should use, who may act and what successful completion looks like. Reuse the shared identity, permissions, task services and action results. Follow [Build an extension](/docs/extend) and [Write an AI development brief](/docs/extend/ai-authoring) when the change needs code. This recipe is a guide to composition; it does not install a workflow or add a new connector.

## What to check

If the meeting is missing, check the calendar connection and source record. If a preview becomes stale, prepare it again. The preparation report identifies meetings; research or transcript extraction needs an additional implemented step. Enter the agreed checklist explicitly for this workflow.

Plugins must be enabled for their operations to run. Hosted infrastructure and connected providers may have costs; AI conversations use your configured model. Keep each human decision required by the action’s policy, and verify the result in the source screen before relying on it.
