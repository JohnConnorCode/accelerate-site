A won engagement needs an administrative handoff. Assign the agreed follow-up and document coordination so the customer knows who is handling the next step.

This recipe uses existing capabilities in sequence, so your team can keep the source context while assigning the work that follows. Start with one fictional example and check its saved result before adapting it to your operation.

<RecipeIngredients id="insurance-onboarding" />

## Before you start

Use an authorized workspace with Client onboarding and Overdue commitments enabled in **Plugins**, a won opportunity, and valid task assignees. These plugins start disabled. Confirm the source opportunity belongs to the customer you intend to onboard.

Explore the [fictional workspaces](/demo/command-center) to see the shared interface. There is no dedicated demo for this industry; the example adapts existing capabilities rather than claiming a preconfigured industry product.

## Complete the workflow

1. Open **Pipeline** and inspect the won opportunity, customer and agreed scope.

2. Open **Client onboarding** and select that opportunity. Prepare these tasks: **Confirm the account owner; request agreed materials through the approved channel; schedule the handoff call**.

3. Give each task a description, due date and valid assignee. The plugin accepts one to ten tasks in a plan.

4. Preview the exact checklist and submit it for approval. Review that version in the shared action queue and approve it when the owners and dates are correct.

5. Inspect the execution result, then open the created tasks in **Tasks & approvals**. Run **Overdue commitments** later to review tasks whose due dates have passed.

## Check the result

Assigned onboarding tasks linked to the won opportunity. The tasks retain their source opportunity, and Tasks & approvals shows their saved state. Checklist creation is the start of delivery; update each task as the customer work happens.

## Adapt it for your business

Add policy lifecycle records through a source extension; this checklist does not issue or bind coverage.

Give a developer or coding assistant the business outcome, the records it should use, who may act and what successful completion looks like. Reuse the shared identity, permissions, task services and action results. Follow [Build an extension](/docs/extend) and [Write an AI development brief](/docs/extend/ai-authoring) when the change needs code. This recipe is a guide to composition; it does not install a workflow or add a new connector.

## What to check

If the source or an assignee changed, correct the input and prepare a fresh preview. For an uncertain execution result, inspect the original receipt before retrying with the same request identity. Confirm the existing tasks before submitting another checklist.

Plugins must be enabled for their operations to run. Hosted infrastructure and connected providers may have costs; AI conversations use your configured model. Keep each human decision required by the action’s policy, and verify the result in the source screen before relying on it.
