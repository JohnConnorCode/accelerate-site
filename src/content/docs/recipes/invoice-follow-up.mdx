An invoice remains open and the account owner needs to know whether to follow up, wait for a promised payment or resolve a dispute. Review the evidence before preparing the next message.

This recipe uses existing capabilities in sequence, so your team can keep the source context while assigning the work that follows. Start with one fictional example and check its saved result before adapting it to your operation.

## Before you start

Use an authorized workspace with Receivables Collections enabled, a configured invoice source and a customer-linked invoice observation. Confirm the source is current before deciding how to follow up. A real reminder also needs a working sender and the required approval.

Explore the [professional services workspace](/demo/command-center/ledgerstone-advisory/today) with fictional records. Use it to inspect the shared screens and simulated actions; configure real forms or provider connections in a workspace you control.

## Complete the workflow

1. Open **Collections** and choose the customer-linked case. Read the invoice evidence, observation time and balance before preparing any action.

2. Review the customer’s conversation history and any payment promise, dispute or hold on the case.

3. Record the appropriate decision in the case: preserve a hold or promise when it applies, or prepare a reminder when follow-up is appropriate.

4. Review the recipient, amount, wording and exact reminder proposal. Submit it through the existing approval process.

5. Inspect the case history and execution receipt. Confirm the provider result before recording the message as sent, and use new invoice evidence to determine whether the balance changed.

## Check the result

A reviewed collection decision with a case history and a checkable action result. The customer decision and any reminder stay attached to the collection case; new payment evidence determines whether the balance is resolved.

## Adapt it for your business

Connect another accounting provider through an adapter before describing its balances as live data.

Give a developer or coding assistant the business outcome, the records it should use, who may act and what successful completion looks like. Reuse the shared identity, permissions, task services and action results. Follow [Build an extension](/docs/extend) and [Write an AI development brief](/docs/extend/ai-authoring) when the change needs code. This recipe is a guide to composition; it does not install a workflow or add a new connector.

## What to check

If invoice evidence is missing or stale, repair the configured source and inspect the new observation. If a send is uncertain, check its existing receipt before retrying. Keep disputes and holds visible so the team can resolve them before continuing reminders.

Plugins must be enabled for their operations to run. Hosted infrastructure and connected providers may have costs; AI conversations use your configured model. Keep each human decision required by the action’s policy, and verify the result in the source screen before relying on it.
