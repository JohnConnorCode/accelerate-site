A customer emails you Monday, texts a question Wednesday, and fills out a form on your site Friday, and if those three touches live in three separate systems, whoever answers the phone on Saturday has no idea any of it happened. Conversations at `/admin/conversations` is where Gmail, inbound forms, chat leads, and transcripts all land on the same thread, the same person, and the same deal, whenever the system can actually resolve who they are.

## What you actually do here

Read the thread with its full history, write a reply, assign a follow-up, and link the conversation to the contact and opportunity it belongs to. Filters for assignment, campaign, and follow-up state keep a genuinely busy inbox searchable instead of just long. [Replying](/docs/conversations/reply) walks through the filtering, sending, and linking in detail.

## How a reply actually goes out

A reply you type yourself goes straight through the communication sender, with an idempotency key and a receipt attached so a retry can never accidentally duplicate it. A reply the assistant drafts for you is different only in that it waits as a staged proposal until you approve it; once approved, it runs through that exact same send service. See [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals) for how that review works.

## Where this sits relative to Inbox and Today

Inbox is intake for whatever has not been worked yet. Today is the ranked subset of everything, across the whole workspace, that should happen right now. Conversations is specifically where the actual reply gets written, once you've decided this is the thread that needs one.
