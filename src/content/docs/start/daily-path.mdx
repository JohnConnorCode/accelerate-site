A customer is waiting for an answer, a newly won engagement needs a handoff, and an invoice is ready to prepare. These are three different jobs, but each follows the same useful path: understand the situation, make a decision, save the next step and inspect its result. Practicing that path makes the workspace easier to use with your own business.

**You need:** a browser for the fictional examples below. Open [Today in Northline Roofing](/demo/command-center/northline-roofing/today), review **Business review**, and expand **How do I get work done?** to choose an example. The demo retains changes in that business’s browser session and labels provider actions as simulated.

<h2 id="inquiry">Answer an inquiry</h2>

A Northline homeowner has asked about an inspection. Before offering an appointment, read the request and the customer’s history so your answer addresses what they actually need. Start in [the fictional conversation workspace](/demo/command-center/northline-roofing/conversations).

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Read the customer’s situation">
    Select a thread and read the latest request. Open the related contact or opportunity when you
    need the property context, prior response or current next action before answering.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Review the reply you want to send">
    Write the answer in the reply field, then choose **Review & Send**. Check the customer and exact
    message in the confirmation. Choose **Edit** to revise it or **Confirm send** to proceed. Ask AI
    can prepare a reply from the same context when the inference service is available.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Confirm the result and assign the follow-up">
    Read the resulting message in the thread and check its recorded status. If somebody needs to
    call back, open **Work** and create an owned task with a due date and the customer attached.
    Return to Today and use **Refresh Today** to review the remaining follow-up.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

**Saved result:** the reply stays in the conversation, while the callback remains an assigned task that the next teammate can open. Reload the thread and task to inspect them again. If a send fails or its outcome is unclear, read the original message result before trying again. Keep an unsent answer available until you know what happened.

<h2 id="onboarding">Start client work</h2>

Ledgerstone Advisory has won an engagement, and the delivery team needs ownership, client access and a kickoff date. Start in [the fictional onboarding workspace](/demo/command-center/ledgerstone-advisory/client-onboarding), reached through **Client work → Client onboarding** when the App is enabled.

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Choose the engagement">
    Select the won opportunity and review the customer and agreed work. Prepare tasks for kickoff
    and the information you need from the client, with an assignee and due date for each.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Review the exact checklist">
    Choose **Review workflow**, inspect the source and assignments, then choose **Request
    approval**. Select **Approve & create tasks** on the proposal below, or review it in **Work →
    Approvals**. With configured AI, you can also prepare the checklist and review its proposals in
    chat.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Inspect the handoff">
    Read the execution result and open the created tasks in Work. Check the instructions, dates and
    opportunity links, then return to Today to see the delivery commitments alongside other work.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

**Saved result:** assigned tasks stay linked to the engagement. A teammate can reload the task link and see what they own. If an owner or source changed during review, correct it and preview again; if task creation is uncertain, inspect the original result before preparing a second checklist. [Client onboarding](/docs/plugins/client-onboarding) explains these controls and recovery in more detail.

<h2 id="invoice">Prepare an invoice and follow payment</h2>

SuperDebate needs to invoice a customer for agreed work. The decision is whether the recipient, line items and terms accurately represent that agreement. Open [the fictional invoice workspace](/demo/command-center/superdebate/invoicing) through **Billing & payments**.

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Prepare the customer draft">
    Choose **Create invoice** or **Use sample invoice** in the demo. Review the billing customer,
    currency, quantities and terms, then choose **Prepare invoice** to inspect the exact preview.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Create and send the reviewed version">
    Choose **Request draft approval**, review it and select **Approve & create draft**. Inspect the
    creation result. When the invoice is ready, choose **Request sending approval**, review the
    recipient and select **Approve & send invoice**.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Follow the recorded payment status">
    Inspect the result and reopen the invoice from **All invoices**. Read the provider’s recorded
    status and remaining amount. When Collections is enabled, open the customer case through
    **Billing & payments → Collections** to inspect any overdue balance and available follow-up.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

**Saved result:** invoice creation and sending have separate recorded results, while payment status comes from the invoice source. Reload the invoice and check its status before telling the customer it is paid. If a provider attempt times out, inspect the original action and invoice before retrying. [Stripe invoicing](/docs/plugins/stripe-invoicing) covers the branded customer view; [Collections](/docs/plugins/receivables-collections) explains payment follow-up.

## What to check

- Check the source timestamp and any partial or unavailable warning before making a business decision. Refresh the review, or open the native record when its source needs investigation.
- Every action in these demos is fictional. For real work, verify the required App, permissions and provider in [Setup](/docs/workspace/setup) before using a customer’s data.
- AI availability is shown by the actual service response. Manual controls remain the starting path when an AI request cannot run.
- Confirm the original result after an uncertain action. [Receipts and audit](/docs/start/receipts) explains what a saved, attempted or confirmed result means.
