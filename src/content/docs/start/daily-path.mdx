The fastest way to understand Command Center is to watch one decision move from a signal to a result. This walkthrough takes about five minutes, needs no account, and every action inside it is fictional: nothing you approve or reject here reaches a real customer.

**You need:** a browser. Open the [Command Center demo](/demo/command-center) and choose **Try the demo** to enter Northline Roofing. You can also choose **Find your business** to explore the six fictional businesses. The interactive example at the top introduces an inquiry, an AI draft and a reviewed result; the business workspaces below let you try the actual interface.

## 1. Find what needs you

Open **Today**. It brings replies, due tasks, proposals, and pending approvals into one ranked queue, each with a reason attached, so you are never guessing why something is asking for your attention. Read that reason and the item's next action before you open it.

The business snapshot opens approvals, follow-up work and your pipeline directly, while **Needs you** lists the records behind the attention count. Choose a saved view beside the heading, use **Customize** to arrange its modules, or open **More** to create, duplicate or delete a view.

## 2. Inspect a proposed action

Choose **Review** on an approval. Check who or what it affects, the exact proposed change, and the context behind it, for an email, that means reading the actual recipient and the actual message, not just noting that a draft exists. A proposed action being on your screen does not mean it has already happened; nothing runs until you decide.

## 3. Make a decision

Approve an action you agree with. If the content needs work, edit it and review the revised version before you approve, rather than approving first and fixing later. Reject anything you do not want to run.

In this demo, a decision only changes fictional browser state. In a connected workspace, approving a send delivers a real message to a real recipient, so read the confirmation carefully before you continue once you are working with your own data.

## 4. Check the result

Return to Today and see whether the item is still waiting on you. Open the action's result, or check [Activity](/docs/command-center/activity) for more context. A rejected proposal is not the same as a sent one, and neither is safe to assume without looking.

## Keep going

From here, use [Conversations](/docs/conversations/reply) to answer a customer, [Pipeline](/docs/pipeline/board) to move a deal, and [Inbox](/docs/command-center/inbox) to triage new intake. You can return to Today between any of them; nothing about this workflow is linear once you know the pieces.

## What to check

- If a real action's outcome is ever unclear, check its status before submitting a second attempt. [Receipts and audit](/docs/start/receipts) explains how to tell a confirmed result from unfinished work.
- Before you connect real data, follow [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup). This demo shows you the workflow; it does not verify a single one of your own provider connections.
