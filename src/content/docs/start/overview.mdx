Command Center combines everyday customer operations with an open-source foundation you can extend. Your team can manage relationships, opportunities, proposals, commitments, invoices and marketing work in the same application. AI can use the available business context to answer questions and prepare supported actions.

For a roofing company, that means the office can find an inspection request, record its callback, prepare the offer, hand the won job to the crew and follow up on billing. A builder can adapt that process with an industry-specific report or App while reusing customer identity and existing services.

## Find the business area you need

- [Customers and conversations](/docs/contacts): find relationship history and prepare a response with the relevant context.
- [Sales and follow-up](/docs/pipeline): give inquiries an owner, manage opportunities and follow customer proposals.
- [Delivery and commitments](/docs/delivery): start engagements and give agreed work an owner and date.
- [Billing and collections](/docs/billing): prepare invoices, offer recurring plans and follow up on selected outstanding balances.
- [Marketing and publishing](/docs/outreach): prepare website pages, campaigns, sequences and supported social posts.
- [Custom Apps and AI](/docs/extend): adapt reports, connect assistants and build the process your business needs.

Core records provide the shared foundation. Optional Apps add capabilities such as invoicing, Collections, forms and client onboarding. Connections supply email, calendar, payments or model access where the chosen workflow needs them.

## Choose your starting path

For everyday use, follow [a connected client lifecycle](/docs/start/daily-path) in the fictional Northline workspace. The demo needs no account and simulates business effects. [For business owners](/docs/start/business-owners) helps you choose a first workflow and agree who will operate it.

For builders and agencies, [adapt the follow-up report](/docs/extend/first-change). Then use [App design](/docs/extend/apps) and a [coding-agent brief](/docs/extend/ai-authoring) to add your own records, rules and interface.

## Work with AI in context

Ask AI about a selected customer or give it a specific job, such as preparing a reply and callback task. It uses registered operations and the records your workspace can access. Eligible internal actions can use bounded permission; consequential actions retain their required review. [Working with AI](/docs/intelligence/workspace) explains the controls.

## Connect your own business

A self-hosted installation uses infrastructure and provider accounts you control. An implemented workspace needs agreed setup, ownership, operating responsibilities and costs. Follow [installation](/docs/self-hosting/installation) and [workspace setup](/docs/workspace/setup) before importing customer data.

## What to check

Use the [capability reference](/docs/command-center/capabilities) for current behavior, then verify the connections needed for your workflow. Custom Apps currently use source development with an external coding agent. General-purpose in-app App generation remains planned.
