Command Center brings customer conversations, business records, and the work you owe people into one workspace. It helps you answer three questions: **What needs me? What do we know? What should happen next?**

Start with one workflow. You do not need to learn every screen or connect every provider at once.

## Choose your starting point

- [Business owners](/docs/start/business-owners): choose a pilot, assign ownership, and measure the result.
- [Agencies](/docs/start/agencies): onboard clients and prepare a repeatable handoff.
- [Developers](/docs/extend/first-change): run the app, understand the code, and make your first change.

## Try it first

[Follow your first workflow](/docs/start/daily-path) in the fictional demo. Find an item on Today, inspect the proposed action, make a decision, and see the result. No account or provider credentials are needed for the demo.

## Use it with your team

If you already have a workspace, open Today and work from its priority queue. Use [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) to check whether the connections your workflow needs are ready.

- **Know what needs attention:** [Today](/docs/command-center/today).
- **Answer a customer:** [Replying](/docs/conversations/reply).
- **Keep a deal moving:** [Using the pipeline board](/docs/pipeline/board).
- **Bring in your contacts:** [Importing contacts](/docs/contacts/import).
- **Review an AI suggestion:** [Approvals](/docs/command-center/approvals).

## Run or extend it yourself

[Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) explains how to explore the code and connect your own installation. [Build on it](/docs/extend) covers modules, integrations, tools, and MCP clients.

## How the pieces fit

A contact is a person. An opportunity is a potential piece of business. A conversation holds the messages. Tasks hold commitments. Those records link together, so a reply or follow-up can be understood in context.

The assistant can help read that context and prepare actions. Review the proposed recipient, content, and effect before approving. [Core concepts](/docs/start/core-concepts) explains the vocabulary when you need it.
