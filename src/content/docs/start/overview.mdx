Every engagement starts with your business: how it works, what it is trying to do, and where the hours actually go.

## Find where AI fits

We learn the operation first, then identify the useful opportunities and put them in the right order. Nothing gets built until the order is agreed.

- Workflow audit: what happens today, step by step
- Opportunity map: where time is lost or revenue waits
- Practical roadmap: what to do first, and what to skip

## Build only what is missing

The right solution is usually small: a focused workflow, an AI agent, an integration, or a custom internal tool. We create it around the systems and habits your team already has.

## Run it with you

Software alone rarely holds. We can operate the sales, marketing, service, content, reporting, or administrative work the solution is built to handle, then train your team, document the system, and keep improving it as the business changes.
