These guides use five words the way the running application uses them. Read this page once, then [install a connected workspace](/docs/self-hosting) and complete [your first result](/docs/start/first-value).

## Workspace

A workspace is one business's isolated environment: its people, records, settings, and connected accounts. The product code also calls this a tenant. After you sign in at `/admin`, **Tenant operations** at `/admin/tenants` is where workspaces are provisioned, membership is granted, and a workspace can be paused. What you can see depends on that membership. A fictional demo at `/demo/command-center` is a separate browser-only sandbox; it never writes to your workspace.

## Record

A record is a saved business object with a stable identity. The first-value journey creates three that stay linked: a contact, a company, and an opportunity. Open the opportunity at `/admin/pipeline/[id]` to see the people, the next action, and the **Verified activity** that belong to it. Ambiguous identity matches are not merged automatically; they wait on **Identity Review** at `/admin/identity-review`.

## Work

Work is an operator commitment with a due date, a related record, and a completion path. Capturing an inquiry creates a task titled `Qualify manually created lead: {company}` (or `Reply to new inquiry` for a website form). That task appears on **Today** at `/admin/today`. Complete it with **Complete task**, or **Snooze until tomorrow**. The same open work also appears in **Inbox** under the Tasks filter.

## Approval

An approval is a decision on a proposed action that has not run yet. Proposed actions sit in `action_queue` until someone confirms them. On Today they appear in the decisions list. Opening one shows **Review before approving**; **Approve** (or **Approve and send** for email, Gmail, or campaign activation) runs the same service the admin screens use, and **Reject** records the refusal. A task you complete yourself is work, not an approval. This is separate from a sales proposal document on `/admin/proposals`.

## Receipt

A receipt is the recorded outcome of something that was actually attempted. Capture writes an activity titled **Manual lead captured** (or **Website inquiry captured** / **Chat inquiry captured**) onto the opportunity's **Verified activity** list, keyed by source and source-record id so a retry does not invent a second event. Completing a task writes an activity receipt and an audit row. **Activity** at `/admin/activity` is the actor, origin, target, and before/after history for those material changes. A success toast is not a receipt; if the activity line is missing, the write did not finish.

<Callout type="important" title="Missing providers stay missing">
Email sending needs a verified Resend connection. AI drafts need a tenant OpenRouter key. Gmail, Calendar, and Drive need Google OAuth. None of those are required to finish [your first result](/docs/start/first-value). Setup Center at `/admin/setup` reports configuration separately from a successful delivery or sync receipt.
</Callout>
