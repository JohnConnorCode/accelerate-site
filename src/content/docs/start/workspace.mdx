A workspace holds one business's records, connected accounts, and settings. Before importing contacts or sending a message, check that you are working in the intended business.

## Access your business

You need a signed-in account with active membership in that workspace. If you cannot open it, ask an administrator to check the account and membership. Having a login alone does not grant access to every business.

## Keep contexts separate

Switching workspace changes the business records you are working with. A demo workspace contains fictional data and does not verify your real connections or permissions.

Your available screens can also differ from someone else's. Optional [modules](/docs/start/modules) may be disabled, and provider-dependent features need a ready connection.

## Find configuration and help

Use [Setup Center](/docs/workspace/setup) to inspect readiness and [Integrations](/docs/workspace/integrations) to manage connected providers. Settings holds workspace preferences. Platform administration is a separate role from normal workspace membership.

For a new self-hosted installation, follow [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) before inviting a team or using real data.
