Now let us walk through each phase in detail.

## Week 1: Discovery and Strategy

Everything starts with a free 30-minute strategy call. No pitch deck. No pressure. We ask about your business, your goals, and where things break down today.

After the call, we do a full audit:

- **Your website**: Is it converting visitors into inquiries? Where are people dropping off? How does it perform on mobile?
- **Your current tools**: CRM, scheduling, email marketing, phone system -- what is connected and what is siloed?
- **Your follow-up process**: How fast do you respond to new inquiries? What happens after the first contact?
- **Your biggest bottlenecks**: Where are you spending time on tasks that should run themselves?

By the end of week one, you have a strategy document that identifies the 3 highest-impact opportunities for your business. These are specific, measurable improvements -- not vague promises about "leveraging AI."

## Weeks 2-3: Build and Configure

This is where most of the heavy lifting happens -- and none of it is on you.

Depending on your [package](/packages), this phase includes:

**Website rebuild or optimization.** We build fast, conversion-focused websites designed to turn visitors into booked appointments. Every page has a purpose. Every section moves the visitor toward action.

**AI chat agent setup.** A trained AI assistant goes on your website, ready to answer questions, capture contact information, and book consultations 24/7. We train it on your specific services, pricing structure, and FAQs.

**AI phone receptionist.** Every call answered on the first ring, day or night. Your AI receptionist qualifies callers, answers common questions, and books appointments directly into your calendar.

**CRM integration.** Your customer data, pipeline, and communication history -- all in one place, all connected to your automations.

**Automation workflows.** Follow-up sequences, appointment reminders, review requests, re-engagement campaigns. We build the systems that run while you sleep.

During this phase, your job is simple: review what we build and tell us what needs adjusting. You will get a walkthrough of every system before anything goes live.

## Week 4: Launch and Test

Everything goes live. This is the part where most DIY setups fall apart -- but it is where having a dedicated team makes the biggest difference.

Here is what launch week looks like:

- Your new website (or optimized existing site) goes live
- Your AI chat agent starts handling visitor conversations
- Your AI receptionist answers its first call
- Your follow-up sequences start running on real inquiries
- Your automation workflows trigger for the first time

We monitor everything closely during launch week. Response quality, conversion rates, system errors, edge cases -- we catch and fix issues in real time. You will hear from us proactively. We do not wait for you to find problems.

By the end of week four, your systems are live and handling real inquiries. You are already saving time. Your response speed has gone from hours (or days) to seconds.

## Weeks 5-8: Optimize

This is the phase most agencies skip. Setup is the easy part. Optimization is where the real value lives.

During weeks five through eight, we are analyzing everything:

- **Which messages get replies?** We test different subject lines, SMS copy, and follow-up timing to find what resonates with your audience.
- **Which pages convert?** We look at traffic patterns, form completions, and chat interactions to identify what is working and what needs improvement.
- **How is the AI performing?** We review chat transcripts and call recordings to refine scripts, add new responses, and handle edge cases your AI has encountered.
- **What is the data telling us?** Monthly reporting starts. You get real numbers: inquiries captured, appointments booked, response times, conversion rates.

This is also when we start seeing patterns. Maybe your AI receptionist handles 85% of calls perfectly but struggles with one specific type of question. We fix that. Maybe your follow-up sequence has a 45% open rate on email three but drops to 12% on email five. We rewrite email five.

## Weeks 9-12: Scale and Expand

With two months of real data, we know what is working. Now we build on it.

This phase is about compounding your results:

- **New automations.** We add workflows you did not know you needed -- re-engagement campaigns for past clients, automated review requests after completed jobs, seasonal promotions that trigger automatically.
- **New channels.** If you started with web chat and phone, we might add SMS marketing or automated social responses. We expand based on what the data says, not guesswork.
- **Refined strategy.** Your month-three strategy meeting is where we plan the next quarter. What is your revenue goal? What is the gap between current performance and that target? What do we build next to close it?
- **Performance benchmarks.** By week 12, you have solid baselines. You know your cost per acquisition, your conversion rate from inquiry to appointment, and your average response time. These become the benchmarks we improve against.

## What Goes Wrong When You Do This Alone

Most businesses that try to build AI systems without a dedicated team hit the same wall around week three. The tools are installed, the initial excitement fades, and nobody is monitoring whether any of it actually works.

Here is what we see consistently with businesses that come to us after a failed DIY attempt:

**The AI stops getting better.** A chatbot or receptionist needs weekly refinement based on real conversation data. Without someone reviewing transcripts and updating training, the AI gives the same wrong answer to the same question for months. A single service area mismatch, for example, can quietly lose business for months if nobody is watching for it.

**Automations break silently.** A Zapier connection expires, a calendar API token refreshes, an email template fails a spam check. These failures do not announce themselves. They just stop working, and inquiries start falling through cracks. We monitor every automation daily.

**No one reads the data.** Installing tools is the easy part. Knowing that your day-five follow-up email has a 9% open rate and needs rewriting is what requires someone looking at the numbers every week. Rewriting an onboarding sequence based on real completion-rate data is exactly the kind of refinement that turns a tool you installed into an operation that performs.

This is the core difference between installing software and running an operation. We do the second one.
