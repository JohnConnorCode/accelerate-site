## How Chicago Businesses Are Using AI

### Home Services: Never Missing a Call Again

Chicago's home services market is massive. Between harsh winters, aging housing stock, and a booming renovation market, contractors are slammed year-round. The biggest problem is not getting inquiries -- it is handling them.

A typical scenario: a homeowner's furnace breaks at 11 PM in January. They call three HVAC companies. The one that answers (or calls back first) gets the job. With an AI receptionist, that call gets answered on the first ring, every time.

**What Chicago contractors are automating:**
- Inbound call answering with AI receptionists that know Chicago neighborhoods and service areas
- Automated estimate follow-ups (a prospect requests a quote, gets a confirmation text within 60 seconds)
- Review request automation after job completion
- Scheduling and dispatch optimization

### Law Firms: Faster Intake, Better Follow-Up

Chicago's legal market is one of the most competitive in the country. Solo attorneys and small firms compete against firms with dedicated marketing teams and intake departments. AI evens the odds.

**What Chicago law firms are automating:**
- 24/7 client intake with AI chatbots that can qualify cases, collect basic information, and schedule consultations
- Automated follow-up sequences for prospects who inquire but do not immediately book
- Document drafting assistance for routine contracts, letters, and filings
- Legal research acceleration with AI-powered research tools

A personal injury firm on Michigan Avenue reported that adding an AI chatbot to their website increased qualified consultations by 35%. The chatbot asks screening questions (type of injury, when it happened, insurance status) and only books consultations for cases that fit the firm's practice areas.

Accounting firms, marketing agencies, consultants, and other professional service businesses in Chicago are using AI to serve more clients without proportionally increasing headcount.
