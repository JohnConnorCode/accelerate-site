## Why PI Firms Lose Cases to Slow Intake

Personal injury is different from every other practice area in one critical way: **your prospective clients are in pain, scared, and making decisions fast.** They are not comparison-shopping like someone looking for an estate planning attorney. They want help now.

Here is what the data shows:

The drop-off is brutal. A 5-minute response window versus a 30-minute window cuts your retainer rate nearly in half. And most PI firms -- even good ones -- respond in hours, not minutes.

The reasons are predictable:

- **After-hours inquiries go unanswered.** Over 40% of PI inquiries come in between 6 PM and 8 AM. These are people just leaving the ER, dealing with insurance adjusters, or lying awake at night worried about medical bills.
- **Paralegals are overloaded.** Your intake staff handles 20 to 40 calls per day. During peak periods, calls go to voicemail. Voicemail in PI is a death sentence -- the caller dials the next number on their screen.
- **Web forms create friction.** A contact form that says "we will get back to you within 24 hours" is telling an injured person to wait. They will not wait.
- **Qualifying takes too long.** Not every call is a viable case. Your staff spends 15 minutes on a call only to discover the statute of limitations expired six months ago.

AI intake solves all four problems simultaneously. Not by replacing your attorneys or paralegals -- by handling the first 3 to 5 minutes of every interaction so your team can focus on signed cases.

## How AI Intake Works for Personal Injury

AI intake for PI is not a generic chatbot with a "How can I help you?" prompt. It is a purpose-built system trained on personal injury qualification criteria, statute of limitations rules, and case-type-specific questions. Here is the typical flow.

<StepByStep>
  <Step number={1} title="Initial Contact and Greeting">
    The prospect reaches your website, calls your number, or sends a text. The AI responds immediately -- under 10 seconds for chat, first ring for phone. It identifies itself as an intake assistant for your firm and asks how it can help.
  </Step>
  <Step number={2} title="Accident Type Classification">
    The AI determines the type of personal injury case: auto accident, slip and fall, medical malpractice, product liability, workplace injury, dog bite, or other. Each case type triggers a different question branch because the qualifying criteria are different.
  </Step>
  <Step number={3} title="Critical Fact Collection">
    For an auto accident, the AI collects: date and location of accident, who was at fault (police report filed?), type and severity of injuries, current medical treatment status, whether they have spoken with insurance adjusters, and whether they have existing representation.
  </Step>
  <Step number={4} title="Statute of Limitations Check">
    Based on the state jurisdiction and accident date, the AI flags any statute of limitations concerns. If the deadline is within 30 days, the case is escalated immediately to a live attorney. If it has already expired, the prospect is informed professionally and given appropriate referrals.
  </Step>
  <Step number={5} title="Qualification and Booking">
    Viable cases are scored based on your firm's criteria -- injury severity, liability clarity, insurance coverage, and timeline. Qualified prospects are offered a free consultation and booked directly on an attorney's calendar. The attorney receives a full case summary before the meeting.
  </Step>
</StepByStep>

The entire interaction takes 3 to 7 minutes. The prospect feels heard. Your team gets a pre-qualified case file instead of a raw phone message.

## What a Real AI Intake Conversation Looks Like

Here is a condensed example of an AI intake interaction for an auto accident case:

**AI:** Thank you for reaching out to [Firm Name]. I am here to help you with your case. Can you tell me briefly what happened?

**Prospect:** I was rear-ended at a stoplight two weeks ago. My back has been killing me and the other driver's insurance is giving me the runaround.

**AI:** I am sorry to hear that. A rear-end collision with ongoing back pain is exactly the type of case our attorneys handle. Let me ask a few quick questions to get you connected with the right attorney. When exactly did the accident occur?

**Prospect:** February 28th.

**AI:** Got it. And this happened in Illinois?

**Prospect:** Yes, on Route 53 in Schaumburg.

**AI:** Thank you. Have you seen a doctor or chiropractor for your back injuries since the accident?

**Prospect:** Yes, I have been going to a chiropractor three times a week.

**AI:** Good -- documenting your treatment is important. Was a police report filed at the scene?

**Prospect:** Yes, the officer said the other driver was at fault.

**AI:** That is helpful for your case. One last question -- have you signed anything from the other driver's insurance company or accepted any settlement offers?

**Prospect:** No, but they keep calling me.

**AI:** I would recommend not speaking with them until you have consulted with an attorney. Our attorneys offer free case evaluations. I have availability tomorrow at 10 AM or 2 PM with Attorney Martinez. Which works better for you?

In under 4 minutes, the AI has established: case type, accident date, jurisdiction, injury status, medical treatment documentation, liability evidence, and that no settlement has been accepted. The attorney walks into that consultation already knowing the case has merit.

## HIPAA, Ethics, and Compliance Considerations

<Callout type="warning" title="Ethics Rules Are Not Optional">
AI intake for law firms operates in a heavily regulated space. Your state bar association has rules about client communication, advertising, solicitation, and confidentiality that apply to AI systems just as they apply to human staff. Do not deploy any intake AI without reviewing your jurisdiction's specific rules with a legal ethics expert.
</Callout>

Key compliance areas to address before deploying AI intake:

**Attorney-client privilege.** When does the conversation create an attorney-client relationship? Your AI must include appropriate disclaimers that the interaction is an intake screening, not legal advice. Most bars hold that privilege does not attach until the attorney agrees to represent the client -- but the prospect may share sensitive information during intake that still requires confidentiality protections.

**HIPAA compliance.** PI cases involve medical information. If your AI collects details about injuries, treatment, or medical providers, the data must be stored and transmitted in a HIPAA-compliant manner. This means encrypted storage, access controls, and Business Associate Agreements with any platform handling the data.

**Advertising and solicitation rules.** In most jurisdictions, your AI cannot promise outcomes, guarantee results, or imply a specialization your firm does not hold. The intake script must be reviewed for compliance with your state's advertising rules.

**Informed consent.** Prospects should know they are interacting with an AI system, not a live attorney. Transparency is both an ethical obligation and a trust-building practice -- most people do not mind talking to AI if they know upfront.

**Data retention.** What happens to intake data from cases you decline? You need a data retention and destruction policy that complies with both bar rules and privacy regulations.

These are not reasons to avoid AI intake. They are guardrails that any responsible implementation includes from day one. ## Getting Started Without Overhauling Everything

You do not need to rip out your existing intake process. The smartest PI firms layer AI on top of what already works:

**Week 1:** Deploy an AI chatbot on your website that handles after-hours inquiries and books consultations. This alone captures the 40% of prospects who reach out outside business hours.

**Week 2-3:** Add an AI phone receptionist for overflow calls -- when your staff is on other calls or after hours. This eliminates voicemail entirely.

**Month 2:** Build custom intake logic for your top 2-3 case types. Train the AI on your specific qualification criteria so it pre-screens cases the way your best paralegal would.

**Month 3:** Connect everything to your case management system (Clio, MyCase, PracticePanther) so intake data flows directly into case files without re-entry.

Each step builds on the last. Each step generates immediate revenue improvement. And none of them requires your attorneys to change how they practice law.
