# Accelerate marketing positioning contract

This is the durable source of truth for public marketing copy. Read it before
changing the homepage, services, industries, Command Center, public assistant,
navigation, search descriptions, metadata, or calls to action.

## What Accelerate is

Accelerate helps a team understand where AI and automation can free up time or
increase revenue, then advises, builds, integrates, runs, and improves the right
solution for that business.

The work is custom. Begin with the business, its goals, its existing tools, and
the work consuming its people. Do not begin with a product and force the
business into it.

The offer has four parts:

1. Strategy and consulting: understand the operation, identify useful AI and
   automation opportunities, and decide what should happen first.
2. Custom systems and integrations: build workflows, AI agents, internal tools,
   and connections across the tools the business already uses.
3. Managed execution: operate the agreed sales, marketing, service, content,
   reporting, or administrative work alongside the client.
4. Training and optimization: teach the team, document the system, monitor real
   performance, and keep improving it as the business changes.

The outcomes are straightforward: give people time back, increase revenue, and
let the team focus on the decisions, relationships, and work that matter most.

## The Command Center

The Command Center is one integrated solution Accelerate can build when a
business benefits from a shared operating layer and durable context. It is not
the company, the default deliverable, or a requirement for working with us.

Other valid solutions include a focused workflow, an AI agent, an integration,
a custom internal tool, training, or managed execution. Recommend the smallest
solution that solves the real problem. The dedicated Command Center page may
explain that product in depth; the rest of the site must present it as one option
inside the broader offer.

## Voice

- Use plain, complete sentences. State what we do, for whom, and what changes.
- Prefer concrete work and outcomes over slogans, metaphors, or named frameworks.
- Be confident without pretending every company has the same problem.
- Use examples to show range, not to imply a fixed package.
- Mention AI clearly, but do not use AI as decoration on generic agency copy.
- Use time and revenue as outcomes. Never invent a client, percentage, dollar
  return, business history, or guarantee.

## Banned framing

- `Same machine. Different Tuesday.` and every `Same X. Different Y.` variant.
- Empty punchline fragments that communicate attitude without information.
- Describing Accelerate as only the Command Center, one system, a SaaS platform,
  an intake service, or a lead-generation agency.
- Treating every business as if it needs the same lifecycle, stack, workflow, or
  product.
- Consultant filler such as `move the needle`, `deployment protocol`,
  `zero-latency architecture`, `high-leverage`, `seamless`, `flawless`, or
  `engineer it out of existence` when plain language says more.
- Formulaic contrast copy such as `not X, Y`, repeated `No X. No Y. Just Z.`, or
  a string of clipped sentence fragments used only for rhythm.

## Review test

Before shipping, a reader should be able to answer all four questions without
inferring:

1. Does Accelerate help decide where AI belongs? Yes.
2. Does Accelerate build custom solutions? Yes.
3. Can Accelerate execute and improve the work after launch? Yes.
4. Is the Command Center optional? Yes.

If any answer is unclear, the copy is incomplete.
