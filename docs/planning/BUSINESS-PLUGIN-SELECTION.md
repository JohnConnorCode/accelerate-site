# Ten business plugin implementation packets

These are ranked product-design judgments for this business runtime, not forecasts. Cash recovery, recurring commitments and delivery control come first; broader internal operations follow. Every card is Later until its dependencies and an approved baseline commit are ready. No plugin is implemented by this planning task.

| Order | Plugin/card key                                                                        | Business outcome                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Prerequisites                                                                             |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | `receivables-collections-plugin` — Receivables and dispute resolution plugin           | Turn overdue invoices into a controlled collection workflow with a clear next action, fewer duplicate reminders, and a defensible record of promises and disputes. Finance observations become work tied to actual invoice balances, not guesses based on won opportunities. | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `plugin-record-view-contract`                               |
| 2     | `retainer-renewals-plugin` — Retainer entitlements and client renewals plugin          | Protect recurring revenue and service commitments by linking agreement terms, actual delivery usage, renewal decisions and invoices. Give the owner time to resolve overages or renewal risk before the next billing period.                                                 | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `project-time-margin-plugin`                                |
| 3     | `scope-change-control-plugin` — Scope change and commercial approval plugin            | Prevent unpriced work and lost margin by making a client request traceable to a reviewed impact, approved commercial change and delivery update. Keep teams from treating an informal chat message as permission to expand scope.                                            | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `client-approval-room-plugin`, `project-time-margin-plugin` |
| 4     | `client-approval-room-plugin` — Client deliverable review and approval room plugin     | Replace scattered approval threads with a single customer-facing review workflow that identifies exactly which deliverable version was approved and what the team must change next.                                                                                          | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `plugin-record-view-contract`                               |
| 5     | `delivery-capacity-plugin` — Delivery capacity and assignment planning plugin          | Give the business a realistic delivery promise by connecting committed work, availability and skills. Turn overload or missed coverage into explicit scheduling decisions before a customer deadline is missed.                                                              | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `won-to-delivery-handoff`                                   |
| 6     | `project-time-margin-plugin` — Project time, billable usage and margin control plugin  | Make project economics actionable by turning approved time and costs into billable usage, invoice preparation and margin exceptions. Distinguish estimates, earned/billable value, invoiced value and received cash.                                                         | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `won-to-delivery-handoff`                                   |
| 7     | `client-document-intake-plugin` — Client document and access readiness plugin          | Unblock onboarding and delivery by tracking exactly which client documents, information and access grants are missing, who owns the request and whether the submitted material is actually usable.                                                                           | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `client-approval-room-plugin`                               |
| 8     | `vendor-obligations-plugin` — Vendor subscriptions and obligation management plugin    | Prevent forgotten renewals, unused service spend and missed vendor commitments by turning confirmed vendor terms into accountable review and cancellation work.                                                                                                              | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `purchasing-receipts-plugin`                                |
| 9     | `expense-approval-plugin` — Expense intake, approval and reimbursement tracking plugin | Give small teams a complete expense workflow from evidence to approval and reimbursement status, with project attribution and duplicate prevention. Make cash commitments visible without pretending to be the accounting system.                                            | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `project-time-margin-plugin`                                |
| 10    | `hiring-workflow-plugin` — Hiring pipeline and interview coordination plugin           | Help a growing service business fill real delivery gaps through an accountable hiring workflow, reusing identity, conversations, calendar, work and approvals without mixing candidates into sales opportunities.                                                            | `workshelter-reuse-baseline`, `delivery-capacity-plugin`                                  |

The full implementation packets live on the Feature Board, including typed contracts, acceptance IDs, exact donor references, sequential implementation steps, edge cases, demo fixtures, verification and non-goals. This document is a selection rationale and index, not a second work queue.

Collections complements invoicing and the finance coworker by acting on actual balances. Retainers own agreement entitlements; time/margin owns approved usage; capacity owns future assignments. Scope changes own commercial amendments; approval rooms own version-bound deliverable decisions; intake owns readiness evidence. Vendor obligations complement purchasing without copying stock/receipt state. Expenses track approvals and reimbursement evidence without becoming an accounting ledger. Hiring uses canonical people but a separate, restricted application lifecycle.
