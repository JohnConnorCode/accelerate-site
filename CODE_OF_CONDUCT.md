# Code of conduct

We are committed to a collaborative community where people can contribute without harassment or intimidation.

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while addressing impact. Do not use discriminatory language, publish private information, pressure others for unpaid work, or turn technical disagreement into personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the safety or usefulness of the project.

Report conduct concerns privately to [john@acceleratewith.us](mailto:john@acceleratewith.us). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to conflicts of interest and the safety of the people involved.
